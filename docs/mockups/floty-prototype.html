Design direction · proposal for your feedback

Floty design direction

floty

Your new front-desk employee. The mark is a speech bubble with a face: the product is someone you talk to, not a dashboard you click through. Lowercase, rounded and a little playful, so it feels at home in a salon or a studio.

Color

One brand color, raspberry, used for the things you act on. Status colors are separate so "needs your OK" never looks like a brand flourish. Every color has a dark-mode twin; all text pairs pass WCAG AA contrast.
