    Pepper design direction

    pepper

    Your new front-desk employee. The mark is a chili-red speech bubble with a face and a green pepper stem: the product is someone you talk to, not a dashboard you click through. Lowercase, rounded and a little playful, so it feels at home in a salon or a studio.

    Color

    Pepper green for the things you act on, chili red as a small accent for the mark and Pepper's own voice. Status colors are separate (done is teal, not green), and chili is never used for status, so it never looks like an error. Every color has a dark-mode twin; all text pairs pass WCAG AA contrast.
