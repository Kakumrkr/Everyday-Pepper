# PCR-007: The V1 re-plan: foundation first, a new look, SMS and deposits in V1

- **Status:** Approved
- **Date:** 2026-10-26 (planning session P1)
- **Plan version:** from 0.32 → to 0.33

## What changes and why
An outside launch-readiness review (2026-10-25, the user's private notes) found the AI and trust layer pilot-grade, and the business foundation around it missing. The gaps were an empty start, no import, no password reset, email only, no deposits and no deploy. P1 checked every claim against the code, and all held. The user wants every scorecard category at 10, and everything owners rely on in competitors' tools plus more. The plan changes as follows (details in the [V1 playbook](../v1-playbook.md)):

1. **Phases 9–11 are replaced by Phases 9–17:**
   - 9 New look
   - 10 First 10 minutes
   - 11 Switching + privacy
   - 12 The employee
   - 13 Pilot readiness
   - the pilot
   - 14 SMS
   - 15 Deposits
   - 16 Calendar + staff
   - 17 Launch gate

   Sessions S39–S76 each have a row.
2. **Into V1:** SMS (with approve by text), deposits, card on file and no-show fees (Stripe Connect Express, smart deposits), password reset and email verification, data export and deletion, client merge and delete, history import, week view, recurring appointments, five reports, review requests, Pepper's own billing, a landing page.
3. **Out of V1:** the old Phase 9's website, Google profile and photo extraction (AC-W1-1, AC-W1-2) move to after V1. The own-domain mini-website and booking-page layouts are staged to after PMF and V1.1.
4. **Interaction model:** cards first, chat second ([ADR-0043](../../adr/0043-card-board-first-interaction.md)).
5. **A complete redesign first,** with design rules enforced by tests ([ADR-0044](../../adr/0044-redesign-and-design-rules.md), [DESIGN.md](../../design/DESIGN.md)).
6. **First customer narrowed** to 1–6 chair owner-operators: barbers, hair, lash/brow, nails.
7. **A scorecard gate at every phase end** ([scorecard.md](../scorecard.md)), and the session standard (playbook §3): product walkthrough, top-up, retro, skills by kind.
8. **AI budget:** $10 for Phases 9–17 on top of the $3.80 spent, with development evals on a separate OpenAI project with data sharing on ([ai-budget.md](../ai-budget.md)).

## Compatibility strategy
Docs only in P1. Every planned change is additive: new tables, nullable columns, new tool versions, a new outbox channel, new modules behind `packages/contracts`. The redesign keeps routes, the theme pipeline and the booking page's brand color, and each screen's journeys are updated in the session that restyles it. Existing accounts are not forced through email verification.

## Tests proving no regression
- Every existing suite stays green in every session: unit, integration (1,239), Playwright (94), the cross-tenant isolation suite at 100%, and the eval replays.
- New in Phase 9: screenshot tests at 3 widths × 2 themes, the raw-color lint rule, and a tap-target check.
- Each session adds the tests named in its playbook row.
