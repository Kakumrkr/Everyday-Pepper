## What changes and why

S45 makes a newly seeded business bookable immediately. S46's original "checklist until bookable" can therefore disappear before the owner checks the template's prices, hours and owner-as-provider assumption. The original S46 gate also refers to closing every issue in an earlier private review's UX #1–#5, although contact/profile details belong to S47. Replace that ambiguous dependency with explicit, shared acceptance criteria.

Source review at `66e5c26` also found that the public slot picker starts on today and shows a request error without an explicit retry. Its date strip covers 14 days; absence of a free time in that strip must not be presented as proof that the business is unconfigured or has no availability at any later date.

The owner additionally requested a maintained workflow for all sessions through project completion and fewer standalone session documents. Existing `/session-close` always invokes the application check even for prose-only work; repeated suite runs and unstable checks add time. Extend this same unpublished PCR with the global instructions below, rather than creating another record just to hold a session brief. The measured CI baseline is maintained in `engineering/testing-strategy.md`.

### Approved S46 behavior

1. **Separate setup review from technical bookability.** An authorized owner/manager sees a concise review of the current menu/prices, weekly hours, time zone and who is bookable, including the owner seeded as a provider. Existing edit screens remain the editing paths. Valid seeded data does not count as human-reviewed merely because it exists. A "I've checked these details" action records acknowledgment per business; it is remembered on refresh and another device. Show the review to existing businesses without acknowledgment too; do not backfill it as completed. No mandatory wizard or delay to the existing fast-signup/booking path.
2. **Keep operational warnings current.** Readiness warnings derive from current services, active staff, assignments and working hours, rather than the historical `bookable` event or the setup acknowledgment. Missing staff/hours remain actionable after review is acknowledged; fully booked dates or temporary time off are availability states, not missing setup. Direct actions respect existing permissions, and staff users are not shown management-only controls.
3. **Make public staff/service choices coherent.** Exclude providers with no configured working hours from public choices. A public service needs at least one active, eligible provider with hours. Do not hide an otherwise configured provider solely because they are fully booked or on time off in the viewed period. Retain internal Team/Calendar visibility and existing permission/domain enforcement.
4. **Open the initial booking date strip on its first available day.** Search within the displayed range using existing domain rules, in the business's time zone. Clearly describe an empty range and preserve the existing waitlist path. A failed or incomplete/truncated response cannot establish that a day is full. Avoid an unbounded search or a cascade of one-request-per-day scans. Re-evaluate for a changed service/staff selection; a late response must not overwrite a newer choice or a date the customer deliberately selected.
5. **Offer explicit request recovery.** A failed availability request has "Try again" for the same selection. Preserve service, provider, date and any already-entered customer details; show accessible loading/error feedback and prevent duplicate submissions and stale results.
6. **Use accurate friendly zone names.** Use "Central Time" and equivalent plain names where accurate, with a meaningful fallback for other zones. Do not silently change the stored zone or collapse zones with different DST behavior (e.g. Phoenix versus Denver). Dates and times still use the business's zone.

## Compatibility strategy

Additive behavior and any necessary nullable/default-safe tenant setting. Current businesses remain bookable under their existing configuration while the review is offered. Acknowledging setup never changes services/hours, grants permissions, turns off warnings or creates an activation event. Existing active/inactive staff semantics, staff-owned data access, booking verification, slot-taken alternatives, manage links, domain booking rules, outbox and idempotency stay intact.

The brief identifies the storage/contract choice and exact affected packages before implementation; no new architectural layer, service or dependency is required by this PCR.

The workflow retains the required code-change suites and all existing test/CI/helper scripts. The prose-only route applies only when no runtime consumes the changes; uncertain/mixed work takes the code route. Existing session logs remain historical records and are not rewritten. The full one-off efficiency investigation is kept in the local advisor workspace; only its reusable baseline and guidance are folded into the existing testing strategy.

## Tests proving no regression

New criteria: **AC-W1-11/12**, **AC-W8-3/4/5/6** in `product/acceptance-criteria.md`. Their traceability entries are **planned**, not passed.

- Setup: seeded and unseeded businesses, acknowledgment remembered on reload/another session, no mutation of defaults, existing-business behavior, permitted versus staff roles, tenant isolation, and reappearing/current blockers after acknowledgment. Keep activation exactly-once tests and seeded signup behavior.
- Public menu: mixed configured/unconfigured providers; a service offered only by a provider without hours; inactive providers; configured staff fully booked or temporarily off; internal staff remains visible to authorized users.
- Availability UI/domain: today full but a later displayed day free; no times in the displayed range; minimum notice, staff eligibility and business time zone; no success/empty claim from a failed/truncated response; changed service/staff or deliberate date selection during an in-flight search.
- Retry: first fetch fails and the next succeeds without losing selections; stale response does not overwrite new state; accessible loading/error/retry feedback.
- Zone labels: Chicago, a DST-observing Mountain zone, Phoenix, UTC and a meaningful non-US fallback; correct date boundaries and DST transitions using deterministic clocks.
- Preserve concurrency/verification-alternative booking and waitlist behavior. Walk the changed owner and customer flow at 375 px in light and dark; review existing wider screenshots as required by DESIGN.
- Follow the existing tiered checks and PCR-008: targeted tests during work; review/fixes before final checks; `pnpm check`, integration when its code changed, and full Playwright once sequentially. No test reduction and no real-model calls.
- Workflow validation: run the existing skill validator on both edited skills; check Markdown references, whitespace and the unchanged acceptance IDs. Review prose-only, UI-only, backend/schema, runtime-prompt, mixed-scope, repeated-failure and unauthorized-publication scenarios against the check matrix. These instruction reviews are not a claim that a future cloud session's speedup was measured.
