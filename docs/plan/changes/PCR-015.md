# PCR-015: S67 splits in two (smart deposits, then the cancellation-policy builder)

- **Status:** Approved (user, 2026-11-24, at the S67 brief: "go with the recommended options")
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Plan version:** 0.40 → 0.41

## What changes and why

S67's row held two features: deposits only for new clients and clients with a no-show history, and a cancellation-policy builder whose text appears on the booking page and in every email. Together they span the booking rule, a settings screen, the booking page, the manage page and four emails (PCR-008). The row is split, as S65/S65.2 and S66/S66.2 were:

| Session | Target | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **S67** | **Smart deposits**: "Who pays a deposit" per business (Everyone, or New clients and no-shows); a regular (a visit that has ended, no no-show in 12 months, by the verified email) books with no payment step; the standing read only after the code; the team's booking sheet shows the deposit in one line (top-up) | AC-X-125…129; the rule's fixture tests; the regular's journey |
| **S67.2** | **Cancellation-policy builder**: the owner picks the notice and what a late cancel or a no-show costs; Pepper writes the sentence, shown on the booking page, the manage page and in the confirmation, reminder, reschedule and cancel emails | The policy shown in every channel |

Decisions at the brief (user): one setting per business, the amount stays per service; "regular" and "no-show history" have fixed definitions in V1 (the owner's two choices are the adjustment P1-11 asks for).

## Compatibility strategy

Every business starts at "Everyone", so every booking behaves as before until an owner chooses otherwise. The api's answers keep their shapes; `payment_not_needed` already existed.
