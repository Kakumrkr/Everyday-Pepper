# PCR-003: Two more actions on the autonomy dial, and how a tool's actions combine

- **Status:** Implemented
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Plan version:** from 0.18 → to 0.19

## What changes and why
The autonomy dial (`AUTONOMY_ACTIONS` in `packages/contracts/src/trust.ts`) was approved with the mockups at S2, with nine actions. S24 ships tools the dial cannot govern yet, and S23's review found two gaps:

1. **`booking.cancel`** (internal write, default **ask**, "just do it" allowed). The `cancel_booking` tool (S24) had no setting of its own. Cancelling is the action owners are most nervous about, so it asks by default. Undo restores the booking while its time is still free and has not started.
2. **`task.create`** (internal write, default **do it and tell me**). The `add_todo` tool (D-3, follow-through) had no setting.
3. **A tool call governs by the strictest of its actions.** For example, a move that also emails the client involves `booking.move` and `message.confirmation`. `decideAutonomy` takes the most careful outcome of all the actions involved.
4. **The bulk lock goes by recipients, not by action name.** Any external action to more than 10 clients asks first (AC-W7-3). Before this change, only `message.bulk` was locked, so a "cancel all of Tuesday and tell everyone" would have slipped through.

Each action now also names the **permission** needed to approve or undo it, and its **undo window** (ADR-0028).
