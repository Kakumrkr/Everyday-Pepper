## What changes and why

S57 (deploy: VPS, a neutral domain, TLS, Resend with SPF/DKIM/DMARC) needs a domain the user hasn't bought yet; the user wants to keep building in the meantime. S59 needs no domain, so it moves ahead of S57 and S58.

At the S59 brief the user asked for support "production ready, like big companies manage their systems", and chose **to build Pepper's own support system** over an outside helpdesk (Help Scout, Zendesk): threads stay in the app, under Pepper's tenancy, export and deletion, with no new processor of owners' data. Done completely, S59's row is five sessions, each one context window (PCR-008):

| Session | Target | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **S59** | **Help centre + support threads, owner side:** `/help` (articles, search, help links on screens, public, readable signed out), "Tell us what went wrong" from More, articles and error states (with context: page, version, setup state; never client data), a public form for people who can't sign in, "Your requests" with the thread, replies in the app, an unread badge and email notices; support email to the team's address; threads in the business's export and deletion | AC-X-13…18 |
| **S59.2** | **Operator console:** an operator role (ADR-0062, written in S59.2: limited, audited), the inbox (business and public requests), replies, internal notes, saved replies, statuses, auto-close after 7 quiet days, the same-day overdue reminder, 👍/👎 after close, screenshots (attached by the owner, seen in the console) | Every support status reachable from the console; operator reads audited |
| **S59.2.2** | *(split from S59.2 at its brief, PCR-008)* saved replies, auto-close after 7 quiet days, the same-day overdue reminder, 👍/👎 after close, screenshots | Job and isolation tests |
| **S59.3** | **Email both ways:** Resend inbound (no new provider), signed reply addresses, threading by address and `In-Reply-To`/`References`, quoted text stripped, duplicates and bounces handled, spam and size limits; tested on recorded fixtures, switched on in S57 with the domain | A reply by email lands in its thread (fixture tests) |
| **S59.4** | **Pilot metrics page** ([scorecard.md](../scorecard.md) §3) on the operator role, first-reply time added | Every metric has a number on a seeded business |
| **S59.5** | **Terms, privacy (memory included) and the AI disclosure in emails** | The pages live with a draft banner; every AI-written email says so |

Then **S58a** (operate, local: backups and a restore drill on Compose, Redis rate limits, a local load test, Sentry/OTel wired to switch on with their keys), **S57** (deploy, once the domain is bought; switches on S59.3's inbound mail) and **S58b** (operate on the VPS: uptime check, alerts, the load test against the SLOs). S60 (security review, pilot gate) is unchanged.

Not in V1, on purpose: several agents with assignment and routing (the model allows it later), live chat, phone, AI answering support (it would change the AI and need a recording), an outside helpdesk.

## Compatibility strategy

Additive only: new tables (migration 0067), new routes and pages, one optional email field. No event, tool or prompt changes; no recording.

## Tests proving no regression

The cross-tenant isolation suite (new tables included, 100%), the export's every-table test, the deletion suite, the outbox tests, and the screens' screenshot baselines stay green; each session adds its own (listed per AC in [traceability.md](../traceability.md)).
