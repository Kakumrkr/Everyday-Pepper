## What changes and why

The user's draft, "Pepper AI layer: session-by-session build plan" (a Claude Doc, 2026-11-26), adds an AI layer after Phase 16. The point is a Pepper that sees a lost slot coming, acts before it's lost, and understands what clients text back. Pepper isn't launching yet, so the layer comes before Phase 17.

Planning session P2 checked the draft against `main` at S72.2. Most of the draft's engine already exists and isn't rebuilt:
- the freed-slot match and ranked offers (ADR-0041, ADR-0056);
- the outbox with Undo (ADR-0024, ADR-0028) and earned trust (ADR-0055);
- `rebooking_due`, `no_show_pattern` and `schedule_gaps` (ADR-0039);
- receipts and the week card (ADR-0058);
- the morning drafts (ADR-0040), texts with consent (ADR-0065, ADR-0066), memory (ADR-0034…0038) and the eval harness (ADR-0027, ADR-0033).

What's missing is what Phase 18 builds:
- a risk score with reasons;
- confirm or release before a slot is lost;
- offers by text;
- keeping and reading what clients text (today any text that isn't a keyword is dropped, `apps/api/src/sms/sms-inbound.service.ts`);
- answers from the shop's own facts;
- a day re-planner, due dates that fit each client and a quiet-week plan.

**The changes:**
1. **A new Phase 18, the AI layer:** S77–S85, S73 and S87. S81 and S82 come in "a" and "b" halves. It runs before Phase 17.
2. **S73 Reports moves from Phase 17 into Phase 18**, just before the gate. It gains Phase 18's own numbers. Without it, the gate's C1 and C12 can't honestly reach 9: both scorecard notes name the five-number report as the missing piece (scorecard §8, §11). Phase 17 becomes S74–S76.
3. **S86, the draft's per-shop learned risk model, moves to after the pilot.** It needs about 500 finished visits and 30 misses per shop, and no shop has them yet. S77's rules carry the risk score. S78's holdback switch stays, so the pilot can measure the confirm ask.
4. **Order against Phase 13 (the domain rule).** Phase 18 is next. The day the user buys the domain, S58a, S57, S58b and S60 go next, after the session in progress. The pilot then starts at S60's gate and runs alongside the rest of Phase 18.
5. **Budget.** Phase 18 gets a **$1.50 cap** from Phase 12's unspent $2.19 (S56b spent $0.81 of $3.00). The reserve stays $5.50, the V1 total stays $10 and the runner's $13.80 stop is unchanged. Expected spend is $0.60–$1.00, from S56b's measured cost of about $0.001 a case. `PHASE_CAPS_USD` in `packages/ai/src/evals/ledger.ts` gets `18: 1.5` in S81a (code, not this PCR).
6. **No training on shop data** (user: "never"). The model is never trained or fine-tuned on a business's data. Development evals already use made-up data only. Production already keeps data sharing off with zero retention.

## Compatibility strategy

This is a plan change: order, one moved session (S73), one session moved after the pilot (S86) and a cap. The later sessions are additive:
- new tenant tables with forced RLS in the isolation suite;
- dial actions that default to ask;
- a new consent wording version;
- system tools outside the chat tool list.

No acceptance criterion or gate of a done session changes. Each session adds its own criteria from AC-X-195 and its own ADR from ADR-0071 where it decides architecture.
