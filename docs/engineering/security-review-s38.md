## Result
No high findings. Three controls the playbook names had no test of their own; S38b adds them (M1–M3). Six low findings are logged. `pnpm audit --prod`: no known vulnerabilities.

| # | Severity | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Medium (proof) | **The daily draft cap had no test.** The morning pass reads at most `DRAFT_LIMITS.perBusinessPerDay` (20) items through `listDraftableRebookings(tx, now, limit)`, and the pass runs once a day per business (`claimMorningRun`, tested), but nothing proved the query keeps to its limit. | **Fixed (S38b):** `packages/db/src/feed.int.test.ts` "keeps to the limit, most overdue first: the morning pass's daily draft cap (S38b)" (the limit, the order, and 0 or 201 refused). |
| M2 | Medium (proof) | **No attack called the system tools from the chat.** `send_rebooking_nudge` and `send_waitlist_offers` are left out of the chat's tools (`registry.ts`), but the hostile fake never tried them by name, so a registry change that exposed them would go unnoticed. | **Fixed (S38b):** `hostile.ts` attacks both as `unknown.send_rebooking_nudge` and `unknown.send_waitlist_offers`; `hostile.int.test.ts` "every attack is stopped by the domain, and nothing changes anywhere": both stopped as `unknown_tool`. |
| M3 | Medium (proof) | **An expired or used "Join the waitlist" code had no test of its own.** The code shares `checkCode` with booking requests, whose expiry test covers that path, but the waitlist confirm was not exercised past its expiry or twice. | **Fixed (S38b):** `packages/modules/appointments/src/public-booking.int.test.ts` "refuses an expired code and a used one, adding no second entry (S38b)". |
| L9 | Low | **Client names in `get_feed` are shortened, not labelled untrusted.** A name reaches the model as a first name and an initial (`shortClientName`, `hostile.int.test.ts` "reaches the model as a first name and an initial only"), which leaves little room for an injection, and the instructions treat every tool answer as data. Other reads do the same. | Phase 11: label names untrusted in every read with the next guide batch (it re-records every cassette). |
| L10 | Low | **Waitlist offer and "taken" emails carry no List-Unsubscribe header or postal address, and ignore `email_opt_out_at`.** They answer a request the client made (joined the waitlist), so they are transactional, like booking emails; the opt-out stops marketing nudges only (ADR-0040). Nothing tests that choice. | Phase 11, with the legal docs: state it in the privacy notice; a client who leaves the waitlist gets no more offers already. |
| L11 | Low | **A claim link is bound to its offer, not checked against an email at claim time.** The 256-bit secret goes only to the client's address and only its SHA-256 is stored; the claim rate limit is per IP (10 per 10 minutes). The playbook's "bound to the client's email" means delivery, not a second check. | Wording corrected here; no change. |
| L12 | Low | **No test asserts that insight runs and the briefing call no model.** It holds by construction: `@pepper/appointments` does not depend on `@pepper/ai` (layer rule, package manifest), and the briefing's text is a template (`briefing.ts`). | Phase 11: a dependency lint rule for the layers would prove it for every module. |
| L13 | Low | **Drafts are refused, not scrubbed, for links.** The playbook says "the link scrubber removes any link"; for drafts the domain refuses any link-like text (`LINK_LIKE`, `contracts/src/drafts.test.ts` "refuses %s in the words"), which is stricter; the streaming scrubber is the chat's. | Wording corrected here; no change. |
| L14 | Low | **Waitlist emails are plain text with no HTML template,** so the "escaped in the email template" control has nothing to escape; `nudgeEmailText` has no unit test of its own (covered end to end by `morning.int.test.ts`). | No change. |

The open low findings of S18, S21, S27 and S33 stay as recorded there.
