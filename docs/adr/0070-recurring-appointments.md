# ADR-0070: Recurring appointments are a series of ordinary bookings

- **Status:** Accepted. Approved by the user at the S72 brief on 2026-11-26 ("Go ahead with your recommendations and start the session and complete successfully"): D-1 split ("My week" and the Phase 16 gate are S72.2); D-2 a preview checks every date, the free ones are booked and the taken ones listed with an alternative; D-3 cancel "just this visit" or "this and following", a visit moves on its own; D-4 one email to the client with every date.
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Builds on:** ADR-0016 (the availability engine), ADR-0017 (bookings, the exclusion constraint), ADR-0025 (the client's emails through the outbox), ADR-0069 §6 and §10 (Google busy times bind through `slotsFor`; one Google event per booking), ADR-0010 (RLS, forced).

## Context
A regular ("every Tuesday at 7 for the next two months") is the commonest booking a barber or a lash artist makes by hand. Booking eight times by hand is slow and misses the week that is already full. Every path that reads bookings (reminders, the waitlist, insights, Google Calendar, the week view, a move, a cancel, the manage link) already works on one booking at a time.

## Decision
1. **A series is a set of ordinary bookings.** Each visit is a row in `bookings` with `series_id`; nothing else reads a rule. Reminders, Google events (one per booking, ADR-0069 §10), the waitlist, the manage link and drag to move work unchanged. There is no RRULE and no "virtual" occurrence that becomes real later.
2. **The rule is small.** Every 1–4 weeks, 2–26 visits (about half a year weekly). The dates are the first visit's weekday and clock time in the business's time zone (`seriesStarts` in `@pepper/contracts`, shared by the api and the web app); a daylight-saving change keeps the clock time, and a time the change skips becomes the moment of the change, as `fromZoned` does everywhere. Monthly and "second Tuesday" rules are not in V1.
3. **`booking_series` (migration 0088)** keeps what was asked: the staff member, `every_weeks`, the start times asked for, the ones that weren't free (a retry's answer), the idempotency key and who asked. Not the client or the service: those are the visits' own, so a client merge or deletion changes the bookings and never this row. Forced RLS: the business's own, and for staff only their own staff member's (as `bookings`). `bookings.series_id` references it within the business.
4. **Preview, then book (D-2).** `POST /v1/bookings/series/preview` checks each date with `isBookable` (the one check behind every booking and move, Google busy times included) and gives the nearest free times of a date that isn't free; it writes nothing. `POST /v1/bookings/series` takes the start times the member settled on (the rule's dates, with any taken date replaced by an alternative they tapped) and books them in one transaction with the staff member locked: each date is checked again, also against the visits just booked before it, and a date that is no longer free is skipped, not an error. With nothing free it is 409 `slot_unavailable` and nothing is kept. Team series have no override.
5. **Idempotent as a whole.** The series' key is unique per business; a retry (or a concurrent request with the key) with the same staff member, rule, times, client and service (compared with its visits) returns the first series with its visits and the dates it skipped; any other request with the key is `idempotency_key_reused`.
6. **Changes (D-3).** A visit moves on its own (`PATCH /v1/bookings/:id`, unchanged). `POST /v1/bookings/:id/cancel-following` cancels the confirmed visits of its series from that one on, each through the same cancel as one booking (waitlist, deposit refund, reminder), in one transaction.
7. **One email (D-4).** A series booked with `notifyClient` queues one email listing every booked date, each with its own reschedule/cancel link; "this and following" queues one email listing the cancelled dates. Texts are not sent for a series yet (SMS is in sandbox until S64.2).
8. **Pepper's chat doesn't book series yet.** `create_booking` stays one booking; a series tool changes the AI's tools and needs a real-model recording.
