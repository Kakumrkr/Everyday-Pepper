# ADR-0071: Visit risk is a band from named rules over booking behaviour

- **Status:** Accepted. Approved by the user at the S77 brief on 2026-11-27 ("Go ahead, start with your recommendations and complete the session successfully"): D-1 `bookings.cancelled_at`, backfilled from the audit log; D-2 the brief's points and bands; D-3 the evening wrap lists tomorrow's high-band visits; D-4 the wrap only this session, the fact ready for S78.
- **Date:** 2026-11-27
- **Builds on:** ADR-0039 (the insight providers and the insights pass), ADR-0058 (proof of value, the evening wrap), ADR-0068 §6 (booking page deposits and cards), ADR-0065 (the client's "C" confirm), ADR-0017 (member scope), ADR-0010 (RLS, forced). Rules for Phase 18: [PCR-017](../plan/changes/PCR-017.md), [V1 playbook](../plan/v1-playbook.md) §5.

## Context
A lost slot is the owner-operator's biggest daily loss. Before S77 Pepper only knew one thing about it: a client with two no-shows in six months (`no_show_pattern`). S78 (confirm or release) needs a reason to ask a client to confirm early, and the evening wrap should say why a visit is at risk, not just that it is. There is no data yet for a learned model (S86 waits for the pilot: about 500 finished visits and 30 misses a shop), and Phase 18's rules are that the domain decides, from booking behaviour only, and that nothing is trained on a shop's data.

## Decision
1. **Pure rules, no model.** `findVisitRisks` (`appointments/src/insights/visit-risk.ts`) takes a business's bookings, the instant and its cancellation notice, and returns every confirmed visit in the next `VISIT_RISK_HORIZON_DAYS` (14) with a band, points and reasons. No clock, no database, like the other providers.
2. **Five signals, named points** (`VISIT_RISK_SIGNALS` in `@pepper/contracts`, `VISIT_RISK_POINTS` and `VISIT_RISK_RULES`):
   - `missed_before`: the client's misses (no-shows and late cancels) over their finished or missed visits, pulled toward the shop's rate as if they had 5 more visits: 2 points from 0.15, 3 from 0.30. The shop's rate is its own once it has 20 such visits, else 0.10. Only scored when the client has missed at least once.
   - `first_visit` (1): no finished or missed visit yet; only the client's earliest upcoming visit.
   - `booked_far_ahead` (1): booked 14 days or more before it. Not for an imported visit, whose booking date is the import's.
   - `online_no_deposit` (1): booked on the booking page with no deposit or card taken (a `booking_payments` row).
   - `not_confirmed` (1): the client hasn't confirmed ("C", S62) within 48 hours of the visit.
   Bands: low 0–1, medium 2–3, high 4 and more. Reasons are kept most points first; a reason holds counts and days only, never text.
3. **Behaviour only.** The rules read a booking's id, client id, start, status, source, booking time, the client's confirm, the cancel time and whether it was secured. A test wraps the bookings in a proxy and fails on any other field read; the contract's reasons are strict objects.
4. **A late cancel needs its time: `bookings.cancelled_at` (migration 0089).** `setBookingStatus` takes the domain clock's instant: a cancel records it (keeping the first one), any other status clears it. A trigger (`bookings_keep_cancelled_at`) does the same for any path that doesn't pass a time (it takes the statement's), and a check keeps the column and the status in step. Older cancels were backfilled from their `booking.updated` audit entry, else `updated_at` (the same rule as the pilot metrics, migration 0073). A cancel is late when it came inside the business's notice (`deposit_refund_hours`, the one notice the policy builder sets, S67.2).
5. **Stored for owners and managers: `visit_risks`.** One row per scored visit (band, points, reasons, when computed), with `tenant_isolation` and a restrictive `member_scope` of `current_member_sees_all()` only, so staff read none, not even of their own visits. The insights pass syncs it on every run: an upsert that changes a row only when its band, points or reasons changed (a second run writes nothing) and a delete of every other row. It runs even while suggestions are paused: risk is a reading of the book, not a suggestion. A client's "C" asks for a run. Rows go with their booking (cascade) and with the business.
6. **In the export and on the privacy page.** `visit-risk.json` holds each row; `appointments.json` gains each visit's `cancelledAt`. The privacy page says what the rating reads, that it follows fixed rules and that only owners and managers see it.
7. **The evening wrap uses the same rules.** `buildEveningWrap` scores the book on read (the wrap is read in the evening only) and names tomorrow's high-band visits with their top reason ("11:00 am · missed 2 of 5 visits"). It no longer uses the no-show pattern; the `no_show_pattern` feed card is unchanged.
8. **A `visit_risk` fact for *Why?*.** Facts hold flat values, so `visitRiskFactValue` flattens a risk (the band, then one key per reason) and `visitRiskFromFact` reads it back; the screens word it "I rated the risk of a missed visit high: missed 2 of 5 visits, not confirmed yet." S78's confirm-or-release card records it.

## Consequences
- S78 reads `visit_risks` to choose whom to ask early; S86 can replace the points where a per-shop learned model wins on the shop's own data, with the same signals and output.
- Changing a constant changes bands for every business at the next run; the fixtures in `visit-risk.test.ts` pin the rules' intent.
- The hourly pass means "not confirmed" and the 14-day window move at most an hour late; a booking change or a "C" asks for a run sooner.
- Old cancels without an audit entry are timed by `updated_at`, which may be later than the real cancel: such a cancel can read as late when it wasn't. New cancels are exact.
- The backfill lifts forced RLS on `bookings` and `audit_log` for the owner only while it runs, and turns off the `updated_at` trigger, so old cancels keep their `updated_at` (S77 review; `migrate.int.test.ts`).
- **Known limits, for S78's brief:** a cancel the business made (a sick barber) counts against the client like their own, since a booking doesn't record who cancelled; and `not_confirmed` scores where a client could not confirm (no text reminders, so no "C"). S78 decides whether to record who cancelled and to score `not_confirmed` only after a reminder went out.
- No chat tool, prompt or instruction changed, so no recording was needed.

## S78: the ask to confirm (2026-11-27; split at the brief, S78.2 the release card)
9. **Who cancelled: `bookings.cancelled_by` (migration 0090).** `client` when the client cancels by their link, `business` for every cancel through `changeBookingIn` (the team, Pepper, a series); `NULL` while not cancelled and for cancels before S78 (unknown). A re-cancel keeps the first record; the trigger clears it on any other status. Only a client's late cancel, or an unknown one, is a miss.
10. **`not_confirmed` only after an ask: `bookings.confirm_asked_at`.** Set when a reminder text asking "C" or an email carrying the confirm link is queued; a move clears it (with the confirm). How the client confirmed is `client_confirmed_via` (`text` or `link`).
11. **"Ask clients to confirm" (`reminder_settings.askToConfirm`, off).** The booking page's booked screen, the confirmation, move and day-before emails say "Please confirm by <24 h before>, or your time may be offered to others" (`CONFIRM_CUTOFF_HOURS`); once that time has passed they ask without a time. The manage link confirms: `POST booking/confirm` with the start the client saw (`booking_moved` otherwise), once, audited as the system.
12. **The early ask: reminder kind `confirm_ask`.** While the setting is on, every visit booked more than `CONFIRM_ASK_LEAD_HOURS` (72) ahead has one, kept out of the night. When due it goes only to a high-band, unconfirmed visit: a text where the consent in force, STOP and the business's texts allow, else an email with the confirm link. `sms_confirm_target` takes the new kind, so a "C" answers it. **Consent (user, in chat):** the ask is a reminder asking to confirm, the same kind of text `sms-consent-v1` names ("appointment confirmations and reminders"), so it needs no new wording; `sms-consent-v3` stays S79's.
13. **Known limit, for S78.2:** a late cancel the team types in for a client who phoned is the business's, so it no longer counts. S78.2 adds "Who cancelled?" to the team's cancel. The cut-off is a platform constant until S78.2 makes it a template default the owner can change.

## S78.2: the release card (2026-11-27; split at the brief, S78.3 the holdback and the cut-off as a template default)
14. **When a card comes: reminder kind `release_check`** (migration 0091). While "Ask clients to confirm" is on, every visit has one at its cut-off (`CONFIRM_CUTOFF_HOURS` before the start, not moved out of the night: it reaches the owner, not the client). When due, `releaseCheck` decides: the visit is booked, not started, unconfirmed, and its client was asked (`confirm_asked_at`) at least `RELEASE_ANSWER_HOURS` (12) before the cut-off (D-2). A day-before reminder that asks at the cut-off itself gave no time to answer, so it doesn't count. The worker's reminder job calls `proposeReleaseIn` (`@pepper/ai`) in the reminder's transaction, inside a savepoint: one card per visit and time (idempotency key `release.<booking>.<start>`), requested by nobody, waiting for an owner or manager.
15. **`booking.release` on the dial.** `external_or_irreversible`, `askOnly` (ask in every preset), `bookings.manage`; on the Autonomy page with the messages, locked with its own reason. The system tool `release_unconfirmed_booking` is never offered to the model, so no chat tool or instruction changed and nothing was recorded.
16. **Approving releases (`releaseUnconfirmedBookingIn`).** Under the booking's lock, at the time the card showed, the same rules again; then the business's cancel through `changeBookingIn` (`cancelled_by = business`, never the client's miss, D-3), the release email instead of the plain cancel ("We didn't hear back, so we've let this time go for someone else", the refund line, the booking page; email only, D-4), a paid deposit refunded in full (`settleDepositOnCancelIn` with `by: business`, from the tool's `onCancelled`), and `requestWaitlistMatch` for the fill loop (ADR-0056). The receipt has no inverse: the email and the refund leave Pepper, and the card says "A release can't be undone" (D-5).
17. **What takes the card away.** `keepReleaseCardInStep` runs on every booking write (from `keepReminderInStep`) and after both confirms (by text and by link): when the visit is no longer booked, the client confirmed, or it moved off the card's time, core's `withdrawPendingProposal` supersedes the card (audited as the system, its feed item expired). It looks the card up with `SKIP LOCKED`: a card being approved this moment is left to that approval, whose dry run then reads the confirm and is refused. The two paths take their locks in opposite orders (proposal then booking; booking then proposal), so waiting would risk a deadlock; skipping means exactly one outcome either way (`release.int.test.ts`, four rounds of a confirm and an approval at once).
18. **Activity.** A release's receipt reads "Released Tue, Dec 1, 2:00 pm", then from the time's waitlist match (`releasedFillOf`) "offered to 3" and "filled +$60" or "nobody claimed it".
19. **"Who cancelled?" on the team's cancel.** `BookingChangeSchema`'s status branch takes `cancelledBy` (cancels only); the sheet asks Us or The client, Us by default. A client who phoned counts as the client's cancel in visit risk. The deposit is still refunded as for any team cancel: what a team cancel does with money didn't change.
20. **Top-up:** the card shows the visit's top risk reason (S77's `visit_risks.reasons[0]`), kept flat on the card's side (`riskSignal` and its counts) and worded by the screens.
21. **Known limits, for S78.3:** the holdback switch (off) for the confirm-ask effect metric isn't built; the cut-off stays a platform constant; an e2e journey can't reach the card on a real clock (the ask must be 36 h before the visit), so the screen is covered by a component test and the gallery's screenshots, and the flow by `release.int.test.ts`.
