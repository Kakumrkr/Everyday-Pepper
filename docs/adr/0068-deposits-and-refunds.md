# ADR-0068: Deposits and card on file: authorize, then book, then capture; refunds by the window

- **Status:** Accepted; built in S66 (below). Approved by the user at the S66 brief on 2026-11-22 ("Go ahead with your recommendations and complete the session"): D-1 S66 the backend, S66.2 the screens and journeys; D-2 destination charges on the business's behalf, the card saved on a platform customer; D-3 a rule per service (none, fixed, percent, card on file); D-4 a refund window in hours (24 by default), the business's cancels always refunded; D-5 the slot is never held unpaid (see §3; this replaces the brief's 15-minute `pending_payment` hold, which would have reached every reader of a booking's status); D-6 the sandbox for every test, a Stripe test-mode probe when the user gives a test key.
- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Builds on:** ADR-0067 (the payments port, the fetch adapter, the sandbox, the business's Express account), ADR-0022 (the booking page's request and code), ADR-0024 (the outbox), ADR-0037 (a job the worker is given, as for episodes), ADR-0010 (RLS)

## Context
Phase 15 promises that no-shows cost the owner nothing. The first piece is a deposit taken on the booking page, a card kept for later (S68's no-show fee asks the owner first), and the deposit back when the client cancels in time. Card data must never touch Pepper, money must never move twice, and a booking must never exist that the deposit rule says can't.

## Decision
1. **The rule lives on the service; the window on the business.** `services.deposit_kind` (`none` | `fixed` | `percent` | `card`) with `deposit_value` (cents for `fixed`, at least 50 and at most the starting price when priced; 1–100 for `percent`, only with a price; none otherwise). `tenants.deposit_refund_hours` (0–168, default 24) is set with the booking page settings. S67's policy builder and smart deposits extend both; the free-text cancellation policy stays as it is.
2. **Terms are computed, in one pure function.** `depositTermsOf(service, refundHours)` (contracts) answers `{ kind: 'deposit', amountCents, refundHours }`, `{ kind: 'card', refundHours }` or `null`; `depositRefundEndsAt(startsAt, refundHours)` is the moment the refund ends. The public business carries each service's terms, so the page says before Stripe's card field what is paid now and what a cancel does (S66 top-up; the words are S66.2's). The amount is computed again from the stored rule when a payment is made; the page's copy is never trusted.
3. **Authorize, then book, then capture: the slot is never held unpaid.** After the code (it counts an attempt when wrong), `POST …/booking-requests/:id/payment` makes, once per request (Stripe idempotency keys by request), a platform **customer** and a **PaymentIntent**: the deposit amount in USD, `capture_method=manual`, `setup_future_usage=off_session` (the card on file), `on_behalf_of` and `transfer_data[destination]` the business's account (a destination charge: the business's name on the statement, the money in its balance), `automatic_payment_methods[enabled]=true` with `allow_redirects=never`; for `card`, a **SetupIntent** (`usage=off_session`, `on_behalf_of`). Starting the payment keeps the request open 30 minutes (the code's ten could run out while a card is typed; shorter than the sweep's hour). Only the client secret is answered; Stripe's hosted field (S66.2) takes the card. Confirming the request reads the intent from Stripe again: it books only when the intent is `requires_capture` (or `succeeded`) for the stored amount, currency and customer (or the setup `succeeded`), else 402 `payment_required`. The booking's transaction marks the payment `authorized → booked` with the booking id; the capture runs after the commit (never inside a transaction). When the time was taken meanwhile, nothing is captured and the same payment books an alternative. A remembered device never skips the payment. Why not a held `pending_payment` booking: every reader of a booking's status (calendar, reminders, the feed, the AI tools, exports, metrics) would have had to learn it, for a state that lasts seconds.
4. **Refunds follow the window, through the outbox.** Cancelling calls a hook the api gives the appointments services (as it gives `notify`), inside the cancel's transaction. A paid deposit is refunded in full when a team member cancels, or when the client cancels from their link before the refund's end, `booking_payments.refund_ends_at`, which is `refund_hours` before the booked start (a team move takes it to the new time; a client's move from their link takes it along only while it hasn't passed, so moving a booking can't win back a deposit already kept: PCR-014, from the security review); a later client cancel keeps it (`kept`). A refund is a `payment` outbox message (`{ bookingPaymentId }`, deduplicated per payment) that the worker delivers with the payments runner it is given (core stays below payments): `POST /v1/refunds` with `payment_intent`, `reverse_transfer=true` and the key `pepper-refund-<payment id>`, recorded `refunded`, `refund_pending` (read again by the sweep) or `refund_failed` (the last attempt alerts). A no-show or completed visit keeps the deposit; S68 adds the fee by approval. **Pepper's own cancels (the AI tools) give no hook:** they can be undone and a refund can't, so the deposit stays for the owner to decide (S68's card), which keeps "money never moves without the owner's OK". The capture is a `payment` outbox message too, queued in the booking's transaction (`payment.capture:<id>`), so a refund that runs before it lets the authorization go instead (nothing was taken), and a late cancel before it still captures what is kept.
5. **A sweep keeps Stripe and Pepper in step.** Every 5 minutes the worker lets go of (`/cancel`) intents nobody booked with an hour after they were made (the row is marked `voided` first, so a booking can no longer take it) and reads refunds Stripe left pending again (an hour after the last look). Captures and refunds not yet asked for are the outbox's, retried with its backoff and alerted when they give up. The definer `open_booking_payments` answers only ids, by the database's clock.
6. **`booking_payments`** (tenant RLS, forced, no delete for the services): the request, the booking (once booked), provider and mode, the intent id (`pi_…`/`seti_…`), the customer id, kind, amount and currency, the refund window as agreed and its end (set when booked), the status (`started`, `booked`, `captured`, `card_saved`, `kept`, `refund_pending`, `refunded`, `refund_failed`, `voided`, `capture_failed`) and the refund id. No card details: Stripe keeps them. Every outcome is audited with amounts only. It is in the export and goes with the business.
7. **Fees.** S66 takes no application fee: the platform pays Stripe's fee on each deposit (ADR-0067 §3). Recovering it is a pricing decision for Pepper's billing (S74).
8. **Sandbox.** `SandboxPaymentsProvider` keeps customers, intents and refunds in its store; `POST /v1/dev/payments/intent` (`{ clientSecret, outcome: 'succeeded' | 'declined' }`, no session: the secret is the proof) settles an intent as a card would; 404 anywhere else.

## Consequences
- No booking page booking exists that its deposit rule forbids, and no slot is held for a client who never pays. Bookings the team or Pepper make take no deposit (the owner's choice); a **waitlist claim** of a deposit service books without one until the claim page takes it (S66.2/S67).
- A deposit service is left off the booking page while the business can't take charges (not connected, paused or rejected by Stripe), and a request for it is refused (`deposits_unavailable`).
- A remembered device still gets the code email for a deposit service: the payment comes between the code and the booking.
- Money moves once: Stripe's idempotency keys on every create, a capture after the commit and retried by the sweep, one refund per payment.
- Platform webhooks for payments (`payment_intent.*`, `refund.*`) aren't needed yet: every state is read from Stripe when Pepper acts and by the sweep. They can shorten the sweep later.
- The real test-mode run waits for a Stripe test key (D-6).

## Built in S66
- Contracts `deposits.ts` (rule, terms, window, statuses, Stripe shapes); migration 0081 (`services.deposit_kind/value`, `tenants.deposit_refund_hours`, `booking_payments` with `refund_ends_at`, the `payment` outbox type, the definer `open_booking_payments`); the export's `deposits.json`.
- `@pepper/payments`: customers, deposits, card setups, capture, void and refunds in the Stripe adapter and the sandbox; `services/deposits.ts` (start, authorized check, attach, capture and refund by outbox message, the cancel's refund or keep, the window's move, the sweep, owners told of a failed refund or capture).
- Appointments: `PublicDeposits` (open, ready, attach, cancelled, rescheduled) and `ChangeBookingOptions.onCancelled/onMoved`; a request stays open 30 minutes once its payment starts.
- Api: `POST …/booking-requests/:id/payment`, `POST /v1/dev/payments/intent` (sandbox only), 402 `payment_required`; the worker's payments runner and 5-minute sweep.
- From the review: the sweep retries a void Stripe failed; a refund refused for good is `refund_failed`; outcome corrections move no money; no client name or email at Stripe; the refund window is stored and a client's late move keeps it (security review, PCR-014).
- Not yet: the screens and journeys (S66.2); a real Stripe test-mode run (needs a test key, S66.2).

## Built in S66.2

A paid request that met a taken time books one of the alternatives with the same payment, without the card field again.

The manage page says before Cancel what cancelling does now, by the server's stored window.
- `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` is required with `PAYMENTS_PROVIDER=stripe`, of the same mode as the secret key, and answered as `publishableKey` with the payment start (`null` in the sandbox).
