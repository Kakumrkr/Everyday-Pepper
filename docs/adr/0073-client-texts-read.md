# ADR-0073: A client's kept text is read by one forced call; the domain decides what it does

- **Status:** Accepted. Approved by the user at the S81a brief on 2026-11-28 ("A and A, go ahead"): D-1 split, S81a builds the reader and its evals on scripted cassettes, S81a.2 the routing and the card's proposal, S81b records once; D-2 the profile time-format top-up stays in S81a.
- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Builds on:** ADR-0072 (client texts kept by the one business they fit), ADR-0061 (the import suggester: one forced call on the routine route, its answer checked by the domain), ADR-0027 and ADR-0030 (evals graded by code, cassettes), ADR-0065 (the SMS channel). Phase 18's rules: [PCR-017](../plan/changes/PCR-017.md).

## Context
S80 keeps a client's own text ("Running 10 late", "Can I move to Friday?") and shows it to owners and managers on a card. Nothing reads it, so the owner does every step by hand. The playbook's S81 asks Pepper to understand the text, with the model proposing and the domain deciding, and with a measured promise that a "no" is never read as a "yes". The text comes from outside and may try to steer the model.

## Decision
1. **One forced call, outside the chat tools.** `readClientText` (`packages/ai/src/client-texts/reader.ts`) asks the routine route once with one tool, `read_client_text`, and `toolChoice: 'required'`, like the import suggester. It is not a chat tool and the agent never calls it: the chat's tools, instructions and recordings are unchanged.
2. **What the model sees.** The text, marked untrusted (`{ untrusted: true, text }`), the business's local date, weekday and time now, and the client's next visits (at most `CLIENT_TEXT_READ_LIMITS.visits`, 5) as labels `v1`…`v5` with their local weekday, date and time. Never the client's name or number, the business, a service, a staff member, a price, a policy or another client. The booking ids stay in the reader and are put back from the labels.
3. **What it answers.** A strict schema (`ClientTextReadingSchema` in contracts): `intent` (`confirm`, `cancel`, `reschedule`, `running_late`, `question`, `other`), `visit` (a label offered, or null when none or more than one fits), `askedFor` (a local date and/or time the client asks to move to, only with `reschedule`), `minutesLate` (1–180, only with `running_late`) and `confidence` (`sure` or `unsure`; a self-reported number would look more precise than it is). A field that doesn't belong to the intent is dropped. Any other answer, invalid JSON, a refusal or a label that was not offered is `refused`: the text stays on S80's card as it is.
4. **Metered.** Nothing is asked without a reservation on the business's monthly budget (`CLIENT_TEXT_READ_LIMITS.maxTokens`, 1,500), settled with what the call used; no budget, no call.
5. **The domain decides (S81a.2).** Only a `sure` reading acts, and only within what the domain already allows: confirm → the existing confirm of that visit; running late → a push to that visit's staff member; cancel or reschedule → S80's card with a cancel or move proposal and free times from `findAvailabilityIn`; question, other, unsure, refused or a visit not named when two fit → S80's card as it is. A text never changes a price, a policy or another client's visit: the reader returns no such field, and the domain takes the client and the visits from the kept text, never from the answer.
6. **Classifier evals.** A case action `clientText` (a text and the client's next visits as times) and an expectation `reading` (the intent, other intents accepted, the visit, the minutes late, the time asked for, and what no request may hold). Two sets: `client_replies` (gate 90%, pass^3 80%) and `client_replies_safety` (hostile and injected texts, and every kind of "no": gate 100%). The runner adds, for classifier sets, precision and recall per label over every trial with a gate per label (`CLASSIFIER_LABEL_GATES`: 80% each, a confirm's precision 100%), and a hard gate across both sets: no trial reads `confirm` where a confirm was not expected. The report adds each set's confusion table (expected by read, with `refused`).
7. **Phase 18's budget.** `PHASE_CAPS_USD[18] = 1.50` (PCR-017); only S81b and S82b record.

## Consequences
- The scripted cassettes prove the plumbing and the graders, not the model. The measured answer is S81b's recording; S81b's gates are the safety set at 100% pass^3 and no false confirm at all.
- Self-reported confidence is coarse. It only holds an action back; it never lets one through that the domain would refuse.
- A client with two visits who writes "running late" without saying which gets the card, not a guess. S81a.2 may pick today's visit when only one is today.
- One more model call per kept text. Kept texts are few (one business, 7 days), and each is capped at 1,500 tokens.

## S81a.2: built (2026-11-28)

Approved at the S81a.2 brief ("Ok": D-1 A, D-2 A, D-3 A, D-4 A).

- **The job.** `keepClientText` queues one `client_text` outbox message (ids only, `client_text:<textId>`, migration 0095) in the transaction that kept the text, when the api has OpenAI. The worker (`actOnClientText`) reads the text, its client and that client's next 5 confirmed visits (from an hour before the text, so a client late for one that just started is covered) from the database, calls the reader outside any transaction, and routes with `routeClientText`. The worker reads with OpenAI alone; without `AUTH_BASE_URL` it proposes no cancel or move.
- **The route** (`routeClientText`, pure): only `sure` acts. A named visit must be in the list; none named: the only one shown, else the only one today, else the card asks which (a visit that just started counts, so "can't make it" never lands on next week's). Late is for a visit today; confirm, cancel and move for one still to come, as of when the job runs (a retried job never acts on a visit started meanwhile).
- **Confirm** (D-2): `confirmClientVisitIn`, by booking id, for that client, booked and to come, under the release card's lock; no reminder needed; audited as the system with `via: text_read`. It doesn't go through the autonomy dial and has no Undo, like a "C" or the confirm link: it is the client's own word about their own visit, and changes nothing anyone else decides. The booking's `client_confirmed_via` stays `text` (it was by text).
- **Running late:** a push to the visit's staff member's devices (`push.client_late:<textId>`), naming the service, the time and the minutes, never the client or the words; no linked login or device: the card alone.
- **Cancel or move** (D-1): `proposeForBusiness` with the AI's `cancel_booking` or `move_booking`, `untrustedContext: true`, one per text; a move only to the time the client asked for when it is free with the same staff member (the visit's own time excluded: `findAvailabilityIn` takes `excludeBookingId`), else up to 3 free times on the card for the owner to offer. Pepper never picks a time nobody asked for.
- **The card** (D-3): always shown; once read it moves to the visit the text was about and says what was done (`ClientTextCardOutcome`: confirmed, told staff, a cancel or a move waiting for an OK, free times, which visit). Nothing is said for a visit already confirmed.
