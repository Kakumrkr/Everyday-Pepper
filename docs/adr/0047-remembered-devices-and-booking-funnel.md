# ADR-0047: Remembered devices and the booking funnel

- **Status:** Accepted (built in S47). Pre-approved by the user at the S47 brief on 2026-10-30 ("Go ahead with your recommendations and complete the session"; D-2, D-5).
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Builds on:**
  - ADR-0022 (the public booking page: emailed codes, link secrets, the `public_lookup` policy and `withPublicTenant`, Redis rate limits)
  - ADR-0046 (activation events: ids and times only, append-only, tenant-owned)
  - [V1 playbook](../plan/v1-playbook.md) Phase 10, S47; [scorecard](../plan/scorecard.md) C7 ("drop-off ≤ 15% at any step")

## Context
Every booking on the page needs the 6-digit code emailed to the client (AC-W8-2). For a regular who books every three weeks that is a wait and an inbox switch each time. The playbook asks for "returning devices skip the email code" and "drop-off per booking step recorded", and nothing recorded where visits stop.

## Decision
1. **Remember the browser after a code proves the email (D-2).** The code step has "Remember me on this phone", ticked by default, with a hint to untick it on a shared device. Confirming with it ticked stores a `remembered_devices` row (`tenant_id`, `email`, the SHA-256 of a 256-bit random token, `expires_at`, `last_used_at`) and sets the token in a cookie `pepper_device`: `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` over https, `Max-Age` 90 days, `Path=/v1/public/businesses/<slug>`. The path makes one cookie per business, sent nowhere else; page scripts never read it, and the secret never appears in a response body.
2. **What it skips, and only that.** `POST …/booking-requests` from a browser whose cookie the business remembers *for the same email* and that has not expired is confirmed at once with the request's own code: no code email, the answer carries the booking (`confirmed`). Everything else stays as it is: the time is checked as before, the per-email and per-address limits count, the confirmation email with its reschedule/cancel link goes to the address, and the team's push is sent. Another email, another business, an altered or expired token, or a forgotten device asks for a code. Each use moves the 90 days on, on the server and in the cookie (it is set again). Without a cookie the mailer is checked before anything is stored, as before; a remembered request needs none.
3. **Forgetting.** "Forget this phone" on the confirmation and on manage-booking calls `POST …/device/forget`: the row is deleted and the cookie cleared, and the answer says whether the browser was remembered (`{ forgotten }`, so the page doesn't claim to forget what it never knew). Remembering a browser again (say for another email) ends the token it held. Inserting a new device clears the business's expired rows.
4. **Who reaches the rows.** Tenant-owned with forced RLS and the restrictive `member_scope` of booking requests (`current_user_id() IS NULL`): only the booking page's own work reaches them, members never do. Both tables are in the cross-tenant isolation suite.
5. **The booking funnel.** The page makes a random visit id (`crypto.randomUUID()`, kept in memory only, linked to nothing) and posts `{ visitId, step }` to `POST …/funnel` each time it enters a step (service, staff, time, details, verify, done, waitlisted), with `keepalive` and without waiting. `booking_funnel_events (tenant_id, visit_id, step, occurred_at)` keeps it once per visit and step (unique, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`); append-only (no UPDATE or DELETE grant); a step outside the list is refused by a check and by the contract. No name, email, phone, address or IP is stored. Waitlist joins share the details and verify steps and end in `waitlisted`, so conversion reads `done + waitlisted` against `verify`. The visit id falls back to `getRandomValues` where `crypto.randomUUID` is missing (plain http). The endpoint answers 204 always (an unknown page records nothing; a failure is logged and swallowed), is rate limited per address (60 per 10 minutes) and needs the app's Origin like every public write. `getBookingFunnel` (appointments, `settings.manage`, in the member's own transaction) counts visits per step over a period, for S59's metrics page.

## Consequences
- A regular books in two taps from their own phone; the owner still gets the same emails and pushes, and the client the same confirmation with its link.
- A stolen cookie lets someone book (only book) as that client at that one business until it is forgotten or expires; the client is emailed about every booking. Untick on shared devices is said on screen. The cookie does not unlock the client's other bookings: those still need their link.
- The funnel counts can be inflated by a script within the rate limit; they are an indicator, not billing. Rows stay until the business is deleted (no personal data); a retention window can come with S59's metrics.
- Waitlist joins still always need a code (out of S47's scope).
