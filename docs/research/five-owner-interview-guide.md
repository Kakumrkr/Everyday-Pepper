# Five-owner interview guide (about 15 minutes each)

A short version of the [owner interview script](owner-interview-script.md), written in planning session P1 (2026-10-26) for in-person talks with nearby owners. Each question tests one planning decision. The simulated answers are in [interviews/2026-10-26-simulated-five-owners.md](interviews/2026-10-26-simulated-five-owners.md). Real notes go next to them.

**Opening line:** *"I'm building a booking app for salons and barbers. I'm not selling anything today. I'd love 15 minutes of your honest opinion."*

**Rules:** don't pitch. Ask about **the last time it happened**, not what they'd do in general. Write down every number they say.

| # | Ask | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What's the business? How many chairs and staff? Who answers the phone and DMs when you're with a client? | The first segment (playbook P1-3) |
| 2 | What do you use for booking, reminders and payments? What do you pay a month in total? What annoys you most? | Competition and the price to beat |
| 3 | How many no-shows or late cancels in a normal week? Do you take a deposit or card on file? Do clients get text reminders? | SMS and deposits in V1 (P1-5) |
| 4 | Last time someone cancelled at the last minute, what happened to that slot? | The value of the fill loop |
| 5 | How do you know a regular hasn't been back in a while? | The value of win-back |
| 6 | When do you do admin: between clients, at night? On a phone or a computer? How long a day? | Phone-first, one-thumb design |
| 7 | **Key question.** Would you rather (a) type or talk to an assistant, (b) tap cards it shows you, or (c) have it just handle things and tell you after? | Cards first (ADR-0043) |
| 8 | What would you never want it to do without asking you first? | Autonomy defaults |
| 9 | Do you have a website? Who made it, what does it cost? Where do new clients find you? | The own-domain website idea (P1-8) |
| 10 | (Show a booking page.) How much does it matter that it looks like your brand? | Branded booking page and layouts (P1-8) |
| 11 | What would make you switch tomorrow? What makes switching hard? | Import and setup |
| 12 | For all-in-one (booking, text reminders, deposits, assistant), what monthly price is a no-brainer, and what's too much? | Pricing (S74) |
| 13 | Can I come back in a few weeks to show you an early version? Would you try it free for 60 days? | The pilot list |

**Afterwards, note:** first name, business type, chairs, current tool and price, no-shows a week, website (yes/no), the (a)/(b)/(c) answer, the price named, and whether they'd pilot. Nothing else personal.
