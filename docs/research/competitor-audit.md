# Competitor Audit (v0.1)

> Method: desk research, October 2026, from review aggregators (Capterra, GetApp, Trustpilot summaries) and pricing guides. **Many pricing sources are competitor or affiliate blogs and disagree with each other — figures are indicative only and must be re-checked on vendors' official pages before use in marketing.** Owner interviews were not possible; this audit stands in for them and should be revalidated with pilot users.

## Competitors reviewed
| Product | Primary segment | Indicative pricing (2026) | Notable strengths |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vagaro | Salon/spa/fitness | From ~$20–24/mo for 1 calendar; more per calendar; SMS marketing, website, processing extra | Feature-rich, marketplace, large user base |
| Fresha | Salon/spa | "Free" subscription model historically; **~20% commission on marketplace new clients** (reported); processing fees | Low upfront cost, marketplace demand |
| Booksy | Beauty, barbers | ~$29.99/mo + ~$20/mo per extra staff; "Boost" new-client fee (reported 30% of first visit, sources conflict) | Strong consumer app, barbers |
| Mindbody | Fitness/wellness studios | Reported ~$99–699/mo, add-ons gated behind sales; 12–36-month auto-renew contracts reported | Class/membership depth, brand |
| GlossGenius | Solo/small beauty | ~$24 / $48 / $148 per mo tiers; seat caps on lower tiers; metered SMS credits | Beautiful UX, solo-friendly |
| Square Appointments | General appointments | Free tier; ~$49–149 per location/mo | Payments ecosystem, free entry |
| Acuity Scheduling | Solo service pros | ~$16–61/mo; SMS reminders gated to higher tiers; no free plan | Flexible intake forms |
| AI receptionist add-ons (Bella AI, Aira, AgentZap, etc.) | Bolt-on to the above | From ~$25/mo | Answer calls/DMs, book into existing calendars |

## Recurring complaints (what they lack)
1. **Pricing surprises and add-ons** — base price rises with each calendar/seat; SMS, website, marketing, AI sold separately (Vagaro, Acuity, GlossGenius, Mindbody).
2. **Commissions on "new" clients that weren't really new** — reports of Fresha classifying QR-code or own-advertising clients as marketplace clients and charging 20%.
3. **Lock-in** — long auto-renew contracts, cancellation difficulty, data export fees reported (Mindbody).
4. **Reliability after updates and uneven support** (Vagaro reviews; Mindbody BBB billing complaints).
5. **Clients forced into a separate app / marketplace** that also shows competitors (Vagaro, Booksy, Fresha).
6. **AI is a bolt-on** — either an extra-cost add-on or a third-party receptionist sitting on top of the booking tool; it answers and books but doesn't *run the business*. Reports note few AI receptionists even take deposits in-conversation.
7. **Reports without action** — dashboards show no-show rate and empty slots; the owner still has to act.
8. **Setup and migration effort** — configuring services, staff and hours is manual; switching tools means re-entering data.
9. **Basic tools for growing teams** — Square/Acuity flagged as basic or limited for multi-staff salon rules; GlossGenius seat caps.
10. **Security basics missing in some tools** — e.g. Acuity reviewers note no 2FA and limited staff permission groups.

## Our answers
| Gap | Our response | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| AI is a bolt-on | The AI *is* the product — it acts through the same domain as the UI | Talk instead of click |
| Reports without action | Operator feed: Done / Needs your OK / Suggestions, smart waitlist | Operator feed |
| Fear of AI mistakes | Autonomy dial, "Why did you do that?", undo everything, editable memory | Trust layer |
| Setup and migration | Build setup from website/Google profile/price-list photo; CSV import from competitors | Zero-setup onboarding |
| Commissions & marketplace | Branded booking page, no competitor listings, no commission on own clients | Identity |
| Pricing surprises | Simple published tiers; usage limits shown in-app (pricing TBD) | Identity |
| Lock-in | Free full data export anytime, month-to-month | Identity |
| Security basics | MFA, granular roles, audit log from V1 | Platform core |
| Weak support | In-app AI help that can also fix settings; human escalation | Platform core |

## Where competitors are stronger (be honest)
- Payments/POS, marketplaces that bring new clients, mature class/membership tooling (Mindbody), consumer apps (Booksy). V1 does not compete on these; deposits arrive in V1.1, classes in V2.
