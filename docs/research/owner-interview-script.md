# Owner Interview Script

Use when you can talk to a business owner (in person, phone, or DM). 20–30 minutes. Don't pitch; listen. Record answers in `docs/research/interviews/YYYY-MM-DD-<business-type>.md` (no personal data beyond first name and business type).

## Warm-up
1. What kind of business do you run, how many people work there, how long have you been open?
2. Walk me through yesterday — from opening to closing.

## Current tools
3. What do you use for booking, reminders, payments, marketing? What do you pay in total per month?
4. What do you like most about it? What annoys you most?
5. Have you ever switched tools? What made it hard?

## Pain
6. What admin task takes the most time each week?
7. How often do clients no-show or cancel late? What do you do about it?
8. How do you know when a regular hasn't come back in a while?
9. What do you keep in your head that the software doesn't know (client preferences, rules)?

## AI and trust
10. If you had an assistant who could do anything on your booking system, what would you ask first?
11. What would you **never** want it to do without asking you?
12. Would you rather text it, talk to it, or tap buttons?

## Wrap-up
13. What would make you switch tomorrow?
14. What would a tool like this need to cost to be a no-brainer?
15. Can I show you a prototype later?
