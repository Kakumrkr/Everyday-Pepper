# Jobs, Messaging & Integrations

> Provides: background processing, outbox, messaging channels, Google Calendar sync, onboarding import · Depends on: Redis/BullMQ, db, contracts · Consumed by: reminders, feed, waitlist, onboarding

## Background jobs (Redis + BullMQ in `apps/worker`)
| Queue | Purpose | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `outbox` | Deliver `OutboxMessage` rows (S19: email; S21: push to one device) | The relay polls `outbox_dispatch` every second and queues one job per due message, job id = message id (ADR-0024) |
| Reminder job (S20, no queue) | Booking reminders 24 h before (AC-W6-1) | Every `REMINDER_POLL_MS` (60 s) the worker queues the due rows of `booking_reminders` into the outbox; the schedule is kept in step in each booking's own transaction (ADR-0025) |
| Due-task job (S21, no queue) | Push a to-do's assignee (or the owners and managers for a team task) when it comes due | Every `REMINDER_POLL_MS` with the reminders, when the worker has VAPID keys; each task once per due time (ADR-0026) |
| Clean-up job (S20, no queue) | Delete expired booking requests (24 h) and sent/dead outbox rows (30 days) | At worker start-up and every `CLEANUP_INTERVAL_MS` (one day) |
| Morning run (Phase 8, no queue) | The daily briefing (S38) and the morning's rebooking emails with drafts (S36a), per business at its template's local hour | A `Periodic` pass every `DRAFTS_POLL_MS` (60 s) over `insight_runs.drafts_due_at` through `job_scan('insights')`; a conditional claim gives each business's morning to one worker; it runs the insights, then one routine-model draft per due regular (≤ 20 a day, metered), each a proposal waiting for an owner's or manager's OK; approved, the email goes through the outbox with one-click unsubscribe headers (PCR-006, [Phase 8 playbook](../plan/phase-8-playbook.md) D-4, [ADR-0040](../adr/0040-rebooking-nudges-with-drafts-and-get-feed.md)) |
| Insights pass (S35, no queue) | Rule-based insights: open time, overdue regulars, no-show patterns, staff overload; feed items upserted by dedupe key, expired when their reason goes | A `Periodic` pass every `INSIGHTS_POLL_MS` (60 s) over `insight_runs`: a business runs when never run, an hour after its last run, or after a booking, working-hours or time-off change added a row to `insight_requests`; one worker per business (advisory lock) ([ADR-0039](../adr/0039-operator-feed-and-insights.md)) |
| `waitlist` | Match and offer a freed time (AC-W5-1, within 2 min) | A `waitlist_matches` row written in the cancellation's (or move's) transaction; the relay scans due rows every `WAITLIST_POLL_MS` (5 s) through `job_scan('waitlist')` and queues one BullMQ job per row, job id = row id; the job leases the row, ranks who is waiting and offers the time through the dial (retries with back-off, 5 attempts); runs only when `AUTH_BASE_URL` is set ([ADR-0041](../adr/0041-smart-waitlist.md)) |
| `calendar-sync` | Google Calendar push/incremental sync | Webhook + every 15 min |
| `imports` | Client import (S48, [ADR-0049](../adr/0049-client-import-batches-and-undo.md): a relay over committed `import_batches`, one job per batch, leased, chunks of 500; hourly tidy-up); onboarding extraction later | User action (commit) |

**Built (S19, [ADR-0024](../adr/0024-transactional-outbox-and-email-delivery.md)):** `queueEmail` in the business change's transaction; `deliverOutboxMessage` claims a message under a 5-minute lease, sends it with a stable `Message-ID`, marks it sent and meters `emails_sent`; retries live in Postgres (30 s doubling to 1 h, 8 attempts), a 5xx answer gives up at once, a message given up raises an alert. The booking page's code email stays direct (the client is waiting); its other emails use the outbox.

**Built (S20, [ADR-0025](../adr/0025-booking-reminders-team-emails-and-cleanup.md)):** team-made bookings email the client (confirmed with a new reschedule/cancel link, moved with a new link, cancelled) through the same `notify` path as the booking page, unless the team unticks "Email the client" (`notifyClient`). Reminders are rows in `booking_reminders` (no content), planned, moved or removed in the booking's own transaction; the worker's reminder job locks the booking, takes the row and queues the email in one transaction, so a cancelled booking never gets one and concurrent workers queue one. The daily clean-up deletes what is past its retention. Both jobs find work across businesses through the read-only `job_scan` policy and are driven by Postgres, not Redis (the delayed jobs and nightly reconciliation first sketched here are not needed).

**Built (S21, [ADR-0026](../adr/0026-tasks-and-web-push.md)):** Web Push for the installed app. `queuePushToMembers` queues one sealed `push` message per device of the recipients in the change's transaction (deduped per change and device); the worker's `WebPusher` (`web-push` builds the RFC 8291 request, `fetch` sends it) delivers it once; 404/410 removes the device and drops the push without an alert. Device endpoints must be on a browser push service. Pushes never name a client: the booking page's team pushes say the service, staff member and time; a due to-do says its title.

**Built (S37a, [ADR-0041](../adr/0041-smart-waitlist.md)):** the smart waitlist. A freed time becomes a `waitlist_matches` row in the freeing transaction; the worker's `WaitlistModule` relays it to BullMQ and `runWaitlistMatch` offers it to the top N (template `waitlist.offerFanOut`, default 3) through `actForBusiness`: a proposal at "ask" (the default), else one offer email each through the outbox after the 120 s Undo grace (dedupe `waitlist.offer:<offerId>`). The first claim books; the others are emailed "taken" (dedupe `waitlist.taken:<offerId>`). The daily clean-up also expires open offers past their time.

**Guarantees:** the DB is the source of truth; jobs carry IDs, not data. Transactional outbox (write + outbox row in one transaction) avoids lost/phantom side effects. Handlers are idempotent (dedupe keys). Exponential backoff, max attempts, dead-letter queue with alert. Redis loss is recoverable: reconciliation jobs rebuild schedules from Postgres.

## Messaging (channel-agnostic)
`MessagingChannel` interface: `send(message) → deliveryId`, `status webhook`. V1: **Email** via Resend for the pilot (D4, ADR-0024), Mailpit over SMTP in development and CI, behind the `Mailer` port (core; `SmtpMailer` in runtime). Templates per message type, rendered with tenant branding and template terminology. Unsubscribe/opt-out honoured; CAN-SPAM footer. V1.1 adds SMS (Twilio, A2P 10DLC, TCPA consent) without changing callers. **Push** (S21) is the team's channel, not the clients': a `Pusher` port (core) with the worker's `WebPusher`.

## Google Calendar sync
Per staff OAuth connection. Inbound: external events → `ExternalBusyBlock` (block availability, details not stored beyond title-optional). Outbound: our bookings → events in the staff's calendar. Source of truth for bookings is our system. Push notifications + sync tokens; full resync on token invalidation; conflicts resolved by "external busy always blocks, our bookings win for our events". Requires Google OAuth app verification before public launch.

## Onboarding extraction
Fetch website / Google Business profile (public data) or OCR a price-list photo → LLM structured output into template-shaped draft → validation → review card. Fetched content treated as untrusted. Client import from other tools (S48, ADR-0049): CSV, JSON or JSONL with mapping presets for Square, Booksy, Vagaro, GlossGenius and Fresha, smart mapping by headers and values (AI-assisted mapping for the rest in S56a), a staged preview with reasons per row before commit, duplicate matching, and undo by batch for 7 days.

## Files
S3-compatible storage (dev server: SeaweedFS, ADR-0011; R2/S3 in pilot): uploads (photos) and exports (imports keep no file, ADR-0049). Pre-signed URLs, private by default, tenant-prefixed keys.
