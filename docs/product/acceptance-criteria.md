# Acceptance Criteria (V1)

Format: Given / When / Then. IDs (`AC-W<workflow>-<n>`) are referenced from tests and `../plan/traceability.md`. Every criterion must map to at least one automated test (E2E, integration, or LLM eval).

## W1 Onboarding
- **AC-W1-1** Given a new owner with a website listing services and prices, when they submit the URL, then a draft with ≥ 90% of listed services (name, price, duration estimate) is shown for review within 60 s.
- **AC-W1-2** Given a draft, when the owner confirms, then services, hours and staff are created and nothing is created before confirmation.
- **AC-W1-3** Given a Vagaro/Fresha/Square/Booksy/generic client CSV, when imported, then valid rows are created, invalid rows are reported with reasons, and re-importing the same file creates no duplicates.
- **AC-W1-4** Given an owner who forgot their password, when they ask for a reset from sign-in, then the answer is the same whether or not the address has an account, an email with a single-use link (valid 1 hour) reaches a real account, setting a new password through it signs every other session out, and the link does not work a second time.
- **AC-W1-5** Given a new sign-up, when the account is created, then a verification email is sent and the person can't use the app until they open its link (a sign-in before that offers to send it again); accounts created before S44 are not asked to verify.
- **AC-W1-6** Given a signed-in owner, when they change their email, then nothing changes until they open the link sent to the new address, and afterwards they sign in with the new address only.
- **AC-W1-7** Given a new owner who picks a business type (e.g. Barbershop) and keeps the defaults, when they create the business, then in one transaction `tenants.template_id` is that type, the template's services (name, category, duration, buffers, price range, rebooking interval) exist, the owner is a staff member offering them with the template's weekly hours, and the public booking page offers times, with nothing typed but the business's name. Every seeded value stays editable; a business created through the API without a type gets nothing seeded.
- **AC-W1-8** Given the create-business screen, then it shows Hair salon, Barbershop, Nail salon and Lash & brow studio first, each with the size of its menu, and the other types under "More types"; creating without a type shows "Pick your type of business." under the field, moves focus to it and creates nothing.
- **AC-W1-9** Given the create-business screen, then the time zone list starts with the US zones (Eastern to Hawaii) before every other zone, and the browser's zone is chosen at first.
- **AC-W1-10** Given a new account and business, when the account is created, the business is created, first becomes bookable (an active service offered by an active staff member with hours) and gets its first booking (by staff, the AI or the booking page), then each is recorded once as an activation event (`signup`, `business_created`, `bookable`, `first_booking`) with ids, kind and time only: no names, emails or phones. A failure to record never stops the sign-up, and another business never sees the events.

- **AC-W1-11** Given a new seeded business or an existing business without a setup-review acknowledgment, when an authorized owner/manager opens Home, then they can review the current menu/prices, hours, time zone and who can be booked (including the seeded owner), with links to existing edits. Technical bookability alone does not hide the review. Explicit acknowledgment is remembered per business across reloads and sessions, does not change business configuration, does not delay booking, and never acknowledges another business.
- **AC-W1-12** Given current services, staff assignments and hours, when an authorized owner/manager views setup readiness, then missing eligible providers and missing working hours are explained with permitted repair actions, including after setup acknowledgment. Editing the configuration refreshes the warnings. A previous `bookable` event, a fully booked period, temporary time off, or a data-load error is never treated as proof of current configuration readiness or missing setup. Staff users receive no management-only action or data.
- **AC-W1-13** Given an owner or manager who has checked the setup review, when they open Home, then the setup card still shows the booking page's link with a Copy button; Copy puts the full link on the clipboard and says "Copied"; staff see no card.
- **AC-W1-14** Given an owner or manager who has checked the setup review, then the setup card says in one line how the booking page did over the last 7 days ("12 visits, 7 booked this week", from the step counts; booked = done + waitlisted); with no visits it says "No visits yet this week. Share your link to get your first booking."; staff see no card.

## W2 Conversational scheduling
- **AC-W2-1** Given Jess is booked 3–5 pm Friday, when the owner asks to book a 2 h service with Jess at 4 pm Friday, then no booking is created, the conflict is explained, and the 3 nearest valid slots are offered as a slot-picker card.
- **AC-W2-2** Given a valid request, when the autonomy setting for "create booking" is "ask me", then a confirmation card is shown and nothing is written until approval.
- **AC-W2-3** Given any AI-created booking, when the owner taps Undo within the undo window, then the booking is removed and any pending customer notification is cancelled.
- **AC-W2-4** Given a staff-role user, when they ask to change another staff member's booking, then the AI refuses with a permission explanation.

## W3 Client knowledge
- **AC-W3-1** Given "Maria is allergic to PPD", when saved, then a fact appears on Maria's profile and in "What I know", tagged sensitive.
- **AC-W3-2** Given Maria has a sensitive fact, when she is booked for a color service, then the booking card shows the warning.

## W4 Operator feed
- **AC-W4-1** Given tomorrow has ≥ 1 h open in a staff schedule and ≥ 1 overdue regular eligible for it, when the daily briefing runs, then a suggestion with a drafted email appears under "Needs your OK".
- **AC-W4-2** Given an approved suggestion, when sent, then exactly one email per recipient is sent even if the job retries.

## W5 Smart waitlist
- **AC-W5-1** Given a waitlist entry matching a cancelled slot, when the cancellation occurs, then an offer is proposed (or sent, per autonomy) to the best match within 2 minutes; the first confirmation books it and others are notified the slot is taken.
- **AC-W5-2** Given a waitlist request whose window still has a free time for that service (and staff member, if named), when anyone adds it (the app or the AI), then nothing is added and the free times are returned, so the person can book one instead.

## W6 Reminders
- **AC-W6-1** Given a booking 24 h ahead, then a reminder email is sent within 5 minutes of schedule; cancelled bookings never receive reminders.

## W7 Trust
- **AC-W7-1** Every AI action shows a "Why?" explanation listing the inputs used.
- **AC-W7-2** Deleting a memory fact removes it from all future AI context (verified by eval).
- **AC-W7-3** Actions in the `external_or_irreversible` tier can never be set to "just do it" for bulk operations (> 10 recipients).

## W8 Public booking
- **AC-W8-1** Given two customers booking the same last slot concurrently, then exactly one succeeds and the other is offered alternatives.
- **AC-W8-2** A booking is created only after email verification; the confirmation email contains working reschedule/cancel links scoped to that booking.

- **AC-W8-3** Given the public booking menu, then a provider without working hours is excluded, and a service appears only if an active provider with hours offers it. Otherwise eligible providers are not excluded merely because the viewed range is fully booked or covered by time off. Internal staff visibility, staff permissions and domain booking checks remain unchanged.
- **AC-W8-4** Given a selected service/provider and the displayed booking date range, when the time picker first loads or those selections change, then the first day in that range with a valid free slot is selected using the business's zone and existing booking rules. No times in that range is described as a range limitation, not as missing setup or no availability ever; the waitlist path remains available. Failed or incomplete/truncated data cannot establish a full day. Late results never override a newer selection or the customer's deliberately chosen date; the search and request count are bounded.
- **AC-W8-5** Given an availability request fails, when the customer chooses "Try again", then availability is retried for the same service/provider/date with accessible loading and error feedback, retained customer inputs, no duplicate submission, and no stale response overriding newer selections.
- **AC-W8-6** Given a public booking or setup time-zone label, then it uses an accurate plain name (e.g. "Central Time") or meaningful fallback while the stored zone and rendered business-local dates/times are unchanged; zones with different DST behavior are not incorrectly equated.
- **AC-W8-7** Given a business with a phone, a public address or a cancellation policy, then the booking page shows each one that is set (the phone as a call link), and the policy on the details step and the confirmation; none set shows nothing empty. Owners and managers edit them with the booking page's color; staff can't; invalid values are refused with what to fix; the mailing address is never shown.
- **AC-W8-8** Given a client who confirmed a booking code with "Remember me on this phone" ticked, when they book the same business again from that browser with the same email within 90 days of its last use, then the booking is made without a code, and the page says so. Another email, another business, an expired, forgotten or altered token, or the box unticked still asks for a code; the token is kept only as a hash, and "Forget this device" on manage-booking ends it.
- **AC-W8-9** Given a visit to the booking page, when the client enters a step (service, staff, time, details, verify, done, waitlisted), then that step is recorded once per visit with a random visit id, the step and the time only (no name, email, phone or address); the counts per step are readable by the business only; a failed record never blocks booking.
- **AC-W8-10** Given an owner or manager, when they add a logo or a cover photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 10 MB; a logo at least 128 px on its short side, a cover at least 600 px), then it is checked by its content (not its name or label), turned upright, stripped of its metadata (location included), resized on the server and shown on the booking page; a new one replaces the old; wrong type, too large, too small or too many pixels is refused with what to fix; staff can't change photos.
- **AC-W8-11** Given the gallery, then it holds up to 12 photos of the business's work (same checks, at least 600 px), each with an optional caption (at most 150 characters); the 13th is refused with "The gallery holds 12 photos"; removing one deletes its stored images.
- **AC-W8-12** Given a business with photos, when a client opens the booking page, then the cover is on top, the logo beside the name (the initials otherwise) and the gallery on the services step, each with alt text (its caption, or "Logo of <business>", "<business>", "Work at <business>"); images come from the app's own origin, cached as unchanging, sized for the screen; a business without photos looks as before; one business's photo can't be read through another's page.

## W9 Team
- **AC-W9-1** A staff user can see and edit only their own bookings and notes (enforced at API and RLS level).

## Cross-cutting
- **AC-X-1** No user can read or write another tenant's data through UI, API, or AI (cross-tenant suite).
- **AC-X-2** If the LLM provider is unavailable, all non-AI screens remain fully functional.
- **AC-X-3** Every screen follows the design direction the user approved in S39 ([DESIGN.md](../design/DESIGN.md)): colors, type, spacing, radius and motion from tokens only; light and dark equally finished; text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 and icons/borders ≥ 3:1 in both themes; tap targets ≥ 44 px; nothing scrolls sideways at 375 px.
- **AC-X-4** A dev-only gallery shows every component of the kit ([DESIGN.md](../design/DESIGN.md) §4) in each of its states, in light and dark, on the Southside Cuts data of the S39 mockups (real names, prices and times, no lorem ipsum); it is off in production.
- **AC-X-5** The signed-in app has one shell: on phones a bottom tab bar (Home · Calendar · Clients · Chat · More) with the current tab marked; on desktop a sidebar with the same places and a "Pepper" group (What I know, Autonomy, Waitlist). More lists the Pepper group, Services, Team, the settings that used to sit on Home, and the account actions. Each place shows only to roles allowed to open it, and every place is reachable in at most two taps.
- **AC-X-6** Every visible tap target on the shell, Home, Calendar, the Clients list, a client's profile, Services, Team, a staff member's page, Autonomy, What I know, Waitlist and the open booking, new-booking, service and staff sheets is at least 44 × 44 px at 375 px (DESIGN D3), checked in the browser.
- **AC-X-7** Every empty state on a signed-in screen says what to do next and offers that step as an action ("Add your first client"), not only as text (DESIGN D5).
- **AC-X-8** The Calendar day view, the Clients list and a client's profile follow AC-X-3 on the kit: staff shown by name and a colored left bar (never by color alone), times in tabular figures in the business's time zone, quiet hairline rows for things that need no decision; screenshot baselines at 375 / 412 / 1280 px in light and dark.
- **AC-X-9** The booking and new-booking sheets, Services (with its service and rules editors) and Team (the list, a staff member's page, the staff sheet) follow AC-X-3 on the kit: each sheet's main action sits in the sheet's footer, at the bottom of a 375 px phone within thumb reach (DESIGN D3); a staff member's calendar tone comes from their place among the active staff and is shown beside their name, never a free colour pick; screenshot baselines at 375 / 412 / 1280 px in light and dark.
- **AC-X-10** Autonomy, What I know and Waitlist follow AC-X-3 on the kit: every on/off setting is a kit switch whose whole row (label, hint and switch) is one 44 px tap target (DESIGN D3); the autonomy dial stays a radio group with a lock and a reason on a level an action can't use; an empty memory or waitlist offers its next step ("Tell Pepper in chat", "Add someone in chat", "Open the calendar"), with no second primary action beside it (DESIGN D4); screenshot baselines at 375 / 412 / 1280 px in light and dark.
- **AC-X-11** The public pages (the booking page at every step, manage-booking, the waitlist offer page and unsubscribe) follow AC-X-3 on the kit in the business's own brand color, with Pepper only in the footer (PCR-001): the step bar and a sticky bottom action bar on the booking page, times as kit time chips, the booking's details as a detail table; every tap target is at least 44 × 44 px at 375 px (DESIGN D3); axe finds nothing on any of them; screenshot baselines at 375 / 412 / 1280 px in light and dark. On Autonomy, a saved change is confirmed by a kit toast that is in view on a 375 px phone; a failed save stays until it is dismissed.
- **AC-X-12** The account screens (sign-in with its code and backup-code steps, sign-up, two-step setup, create business and the invite page) follow AC-X-3 on the kit: one screen title, kit fields with a visible label and their hint and error tied to them, and the main action in a sticky bar at the bottom of a 375 px phone (DESIGN D3); every tap target is at least 44 × 44 px at 375 px; axe finds nothing on any of them; screenshot baselines at 375 / 412 / 1280 px in light and dark. No screen uses the stylesheet from before the kit: `globals.css` keeps only the page's base, and the old token names (`LEGACY_ALIASES`) and `chipClass` are gone.
