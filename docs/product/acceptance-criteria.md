# Acceptance Criteria (V1)

Format: Given / When / Then. IDs (`AC-W<workflow>-<n>`) are referenced from tests and `../plan/traceability.md`. Every criterion must map to at least one automated test (E2E, integration, or LLM eval).

## W1 Onboarding
- **AC-W1-1** Given a new owner with a website listing services and prices, when they submit the URL, then a draft with ≥ 90% of listed services (name, price, duration estimate) is shown for review within 60 s.
- **AC-W1-2** Given a draft, when the owner confirms, then services, hours and staff are created and nothing is created before confirmation.
- **AC-W1-3** Given a client export from Square, Booksy, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Fresha, a generic file or an app's own database export (CSV, JSON or JSONL, at most 5 MB and 10,000 rows), when it is imported, then valid rows become clients (or fill the empty email/phone of the client they match: same external id, email, or a phone only one client has), invalid rows are reported with their row number and what to fix, nothing is written before the owner commits, and re-importing the same file creates no duplicates.
- **AC-W1-4** Given an owner who forgot their password, when they ask for a reset from sign-in, then the answer is the same whether or not the address has an account, an email with a single-use link (valid 1 hour) reaches a real account, setting a new password through it signs every other session out, and the link does not work a second time.
- **AC-W1-5** Given a new sign-up, when the account is created, then a verification email is sent and the person can't use the app until they open its link (a sign-in before that offers to send it again); accounts created before S44 are not asked to verify.
- **AC-W1-6** Given a signed-in owner, when they change their email, then nothing changes until they open the link sent to the new address, and afterwards they sign in with the new address only.
- **AC-W1-7** Given a new owner who picks a business type (e.g. Barbershop) and keeps the defaults, when they create the business, then in one transaction `tenants.template_id` is that type, the template's services (name, category, duration, buffers, price range, rebooking interval) exist, the owner is a staff member offering them with the template's weekly hours, and the public booking page offers times, with nothing typed but the business's name. Every seeded value stays editable; a business created through the API without a type gets nothing seeded.
- **AC-W1-8** Given the create-business screen, then it shows Hair salon, Barbershop, Nail salon and Lash & brow studio first, each with the size of its menu, and the other types under "More types"; creating without a type shows "Pick your type of business." under the field, moves focus to it and creates nothing.
- **AC-W1-9** Given the create-business screen, then the time zone list starts with the US zones (Eastern to Hawaii) before every other zone, and the browser's zone is chosen at first.
- **AC-W1-10** Given a new account and business, when the account is created, the business is created, first becomes bookable (an active service offered by an active staff member with hours) and gets its first booking (by staff, the AI or the booking page), then each is recorded once as an activation event (`signup`, `business_created`, `bookable`, `first_booking`) with ids, kind and time only: no names, emails or phones. A failure to record never stops the sign-up, and another business never sees the events.

- **AC-W1-11** Given a new seeded business or an existing business without a setup-review acknowledgment, when an authorized owner/manager opens Home, then they can review the current menu/prices, hours, time zone and who can be booked (including the seeded owner), with links to existing edits. Technical bookability alone does not hide the review. Explicit acknowledgment is remembered per business across reloads and sessions, does not change business configuration, does not delay booking, and never acknowledges another business.
- **AC-W1-12** Given current services, staff assignments and hours, when an authorized owner/manager views setup readiness, then missing eligible providers and missing working hours are explained with permitted repair actions, including after setup acknowledgment. Editing the configuration refreshes the warnings. A previous `bookable` event, a fully booked period, temporary time off, or a data-load error is never treated as proof of current configuration readiness or missing setup. Staff users receive no management-only action or data.
- **AC-W1-13** Given an owner or manager who has checked the setup review, when they open Home, then the setup card still shows the booking page's link with a Copy button; Copy puts the full link on the clipboard and says "Copied"; staff see no card.
- **AC-W1-14** Given an owner or manager who has checked the setup review, then the setup card says in one line how the booking page did over the last 7 days ("12 visits, 7 booked this week", from the step counts; booked = done + waitlisted); with no visits it says "No visits yet this week. Share your link to get your first booking."; staff see no card.
- **AC-W1-15** Given a new owner on a 375 px phone, when they sign up, confirm their email, pick their business type, check the setup review, add a cover photo and a logo and copy their booking link, then a client can book on the page and the owner's time for the whole journey, summed from the written per-action owner time model (`packages/testing/src/owner-time.ts`), is under 10 minutes; the test reports the modelled total per step and the wall clock.
- **AC-W1-16** Given an owner or manager whose booking page has no cover photo, then the setup card suggests "Add photos" with a link to the Photos card on Services, before and after the setup check; once a cover exists the suggestion is gone; it is a suggestion, never a readiness warning, and never blocks booking; staff see no card.
- **AC-W1-17** Given an uploaded client file, then Pepper suggests the column mapping: a known tool's export is recognised by its headers and mapped by its preset; other columns are mapped from their header names and, failing that, from their values (emails, phones); columns Pepper can't import yet (notes, and a client file's visit summaries such as "Last Visit") are named as such (S49: birthdays and tags are imported); the owner may change the mapping and the preview follows it.
- **AC-W1-18** Given a staged import, then its preview counts the rows to create, to merge, unchanged, invalid and skipped (a duplicate of an earlier row in the file, or a phone several clients share), each invalid or skipped row with its reason, before anything is written.
- **AC-W1-19** Given a finished import, when an owner or manager undoes it within 7 days, then the clients it created are removed (except those who have a booking or waitlist entry since, which are kept and counted), the emails and phones it filled are cleared again where still unchanged, and a second undo or an undo after 7 days changes nothing.
- **AC-W1-20** Given an owner or manager who has checked the setup review, then Home's setup card offers "Bring your clients", leading to the import, until an import has finished and not been undone (S48.2).
- **AC-W1-21** Given a staged import, then its preview opens with the counts as one sentence ("143 new clients, 12 to fill in, 3 to fix", with the duplicates left out and the clients already here only when there are any) and lists the rows to fix first, each with its reason; Import is the one main action and stays off while the mapping has a problem (S48.2).
- **AC-W1-22** Given a visits file (one row per appointment: a date, a client and usually a service and a staff member; the five tools' appointment exports recognised by their headers), then its rows plan as visits: each finds its client as a client file's row would (several rows of one client are one client, a client not found is created), a service by its name (one Pepper doesn't have comes in as a hidden service, named in the preview) and a staff member by name (one not on the team goes to the owner's own calendar, named in the preview); times are read in the business's time zone (S49).
- **AC-W1-23** Given a visits file, then rows before now become past visits (completed, or no-show when the file says so) that the client's history, Pepper's suggestions and the AI's `find_client_bookings` read as any visit, and are never checked against the calendar; cancelled rows and a visit already in Pepper (same client, same start) are not imported again (S49).
- **AC-W1-24** Given a visits file, then rows from now on become confirmed upcoming appointments, except one whose time overlaps another booking of its staff member (already in Pepper or earlier in the file), which is left out as a conflict with the row, the time and the reason, counted apart; an import sends no email, plans no reminder and asks for no confirmation, now or later, for any appointment it brought (S49).
- **AC-W1-25** Given a client or visits file with birthdays and tags, then a new client gets them, and a client it matches gets a birthday only if they have none and only the tags they don't have yet; a birthday may lack the year; a birthday that can't be read is left out with a reason, never blocking the row; an undo removes what it added where unchanged, and the bookings it made that nobody changed since (S49).
- **AC-W1-26** Given a file of 1,000 clients and 5,000 visits, then staging and running it take under 2 minutes together, with exact counts of new clients, past visits, upcoming appointments, conflicts and rows to fix; and the Clients list filters to one import's new clients (`/v1/clients?import=`; the screen's "See your clients" in S49.2) (S49).
- **AC-W1-27** Given an owner or manager on the import screens with a visits file, then the steps speak of visits: the preview opens with one sentence of past visits, upcoming appointments, new clients and rows to fix (the times taken, rows left out and visits already here only when there are any), names its notices (a hidden service it brings, a staff member not on the team), shows each visit's date (with the year, in the business's time zone), service and staff member, lists the times taken apart from the rows to fix, and Import counts the visits; the result says what was added and that no one was emailed (the times taken are its own list, AC-W1-29), and the undo question names the visits and clients it takes back (S49.2).
- **AC-W1-28** Given a client with tags or a birthday, then their profile shows the birthday ("March 12", with the year when known) and the tags; a client with neither shows neither (S49.2; editing them in S50).
- **AC-W1-29** Given a finished import, then "See your clients" opens the Clients list filtered to the clients that import added (a search stays inside the filter; "Show everyone" leaves it; an import that added none opens the whole list), and the result lists each upcoming visit left out because its time was taken (the client, the time, the service and staff member) with "Book again", which opens that client's profile and its Book (S49.2, the S49 retro top-up).
- **AC-W1-30** Given an owner or manager on a client's profile, then "Merge with…" lets them find another client, shows what moves (visits, waitlist entries, notes) and that the other is removed, and Merge keeps this client with both histories (empty email, phone and birthday filled from the other, tags combined); two clients with visits at the same time can't be merged; it is audited as `client.merged` with counts only; staff see no merge and the api refuses them (S50, ADR-0051).
- **AC-W1-31** Given an owner or manager deleting a client, then a client with upcoming visits is refused with what to do; otherwise the confirm says what stays (past visits, as "Deleted client") and what is forgotten, and afterwards the client is in no list, search or contact lookup and can't be booked; their notes, the episodes naming them, their name, email and phone in stored chats, their suggestions, waitlist entries and remembered browsers are gone; it is audited as `client.deleted` with counts only; staff can't (S50, ADR-0051).
- **AC-W1-32** Given an owner or manager editing a client, then tags (comma-separated, no repeats) and the birthday (month, day, optional year) can be set and cleared on the profile; an edit that leaves them out keeps them, and the audit entry names them when they change (S50, the S49.2 D-1 follow-up).
- **AC-W1-33** Given the import result's "Book again" for a time taken, then Book opens on the client's profile with the visit's service and staff member already chosen (by name; the defaults when either is no longer bookable) (S50, the S49.2 retro top-up).
- **AC-W1-34** Given a staff member booking for themselves who finds no client by the email or phone they typed, then "New client" takes a name (the contact they typed already filled in, both optional) and books the walk-in in one step: the client and the booking are created together or not at all, the client is theirs through that booking (other staff don't see them), no client is ever created without a booking (the database refuses it), and a walk-in whose email (or, without one, whose only matching phone) is already a client's books that client instead of adding a second; audited as `client.created` with `walkIn` and the source only (S50.2, ADR-0052).
- **AC-W1-35** Given a business with more than 50 clients, then the Clients list shows them 50 at a time by name with "Show more" until the last one (no repeats or gaps when names are the same), a search pages the same way and also finds a client by part of their phone number in any format ("555 123", "(555) 123-4567"), and the address never carries a client's name, email or phone (S50.2).
- **AC-W1-36** Given a deleted client's past visit, then the calendar and the booking sheet name it "Deleted client" (not "Client"), with no link to a profile that no longer opens (S50.2, the S50 retro top-up).
- **AC-W1-37** Given the business's owner on "Your data" (More → Account), then Download gives one ZIP of everything the business holds in Pepper: `clients.csv` and `visits.csv` in Pepper's own columns (any spreadsheet opens them; a cell that a spreadsheet would run as a formula is written as text), the business, team, hours, rules, services, clients, appointments, waitlist and what Pepper knows as JSON, and a `README.txt` naming each file; managers and staff can't download it (the screen and the endpoint refuse them); it is audited as `business.exported` with counts only, and no copy is kept on the server (S51, ADR-0053).
- **AC-W1-38** Given an export's `clients.csv` and `visits.csv`, then each imports through Clients → Import with every column already mapped: they are recognised as Pepper's own files: into a new business they give the same clients and the same visits (past, missed and upcoming; cancelled ones are listed in the file but, as from any tool, not imported; a deleted client's visits stay in `appointments.json` only, so no "Deleted client" is created), each visit finding the client `clients.csv` brought; into the same business they add nothing, even for a client with only a name, and a value the file guarded against spreadsheet formulas comes back without the guard's `'` (S51, ADR-0053; the round trip of AC-W1-3). A file holds at most 10,000 rows (`visits-2.csv` follows), so each imports whole.
- **AC-W1-39** Given a client whose name has a word that starts with a digit or a symbol, then their initials skip it ("Client 42" → "C", "Dee Hart" → "DH", "4 Seasons" → "S"), and a name with no letter shows none (S51, the S50.2 retro top-up).
- **AC-W1-40** Given the business's owner on "Delete business" (More → Your data), then the screen offers "Download your data" first, and the business is deleted only after they type its name: it is scheduled for 30 days later (audited as `business.deletion_scheduled`), its booking page stops taking bookings at once, Pepper sends its clients no more emails, and the team can keep working in it until the day; the screen shows the day and "Keep the business" cancels it (audited as `business.deletion_cancelled`). Managers and staff can't schedule or cancel it (the screen and the endpoint refuse them) (S51.2, ADR-0054).
- **AC-W1-41** Given a business whose 30 days have passed, then the worker deletes its photos from storage and then the business: no row of it is left in any table that holds a `tenant_id` (checked against every such table), and its team's sessions no longer open it. The database refuses to delete a business that isn't scheduled or whose day hasn't come (S51.2, ADR-0054).
- **AC-W1-42** Given a signed-in person on "Delete account" (More → Account), then their password deletes the account 30 days later: every other session ends at once, "Keep my account" cancels it until the day, and after it the account, its sign-in, sessions, second factor and memberships are gone, and the business records it touched name nobody (actor ids set to none, open invites to its email removed). The only owner of a business that isn't being deleted is told to delete the business first; a wrong password is refused and the attempts are limited (S51.2, ADR-0054).
- **AC-W1-43** Given a change of email that both addresses confirmed, then every other session of the account ends, and the browser that confirmed it stays signed in (S51.2, L15).
- **AC-W1-44** Given an export's `README.txt`, then it names the business's time zone in words, as the app does ("Eastern Time"; a zone without a plain name by its city, "Paris time"), never by its zone id (S51.2, the S51 retro top-up).
- **AC-W1-45** Given a business that is being deleted, then Home says so in one quiet line with the day ("Pepper deletes Fade Room on Friday, December 4."): its owner gets "Keep Fade Room", which cancels the deletion there and then, and everyone else reads that its booking page is closed until then (S52, the S51.2 retro top-up).

## W2 Conversational scheduling
- **AC-W2-1** Given Jess is booked 3–5 pm Friday, when the owner asks to book a 2 h service with Jess at 4 pm Friday, then no booking is created, the conflict is explained, and the 3 nearest valid slots are offered as a slot-picker card.
- **AC-W2-2** Given a valid request, when the autonomy setting for "create booking" is "ask me", then a confirmation card is shown and nothing is written until approval.
- **AC-W2-3** Given any AI-created booking, when the owner taps Undo within the undo window, then the booking is removed and any pending customer notification is cancelled.
- **AC-W2-4** Given a staff-role user, when they ask to change another staff member's booking, then the AI refuses with a permission explanation.

## W3 Client knowledge
- **AC-W3-1** Given "Maria is allergic to PPD", when saved, then a fact appears on Maria's profile and in "What I know", tagged sensitive.
- **AC-W3-2** Given Maria has a sensitive fact, when she is booked for a color service, then the booking card shows the warning.

## W4 Operator feed
- **AC-W4-1** Given tomorrow has ≥ 1 h open in a staff schedule and ≥ 1 overdue regular eligible for it, when the daily briefing runs, then a suggestion with a drafted email appears under "Needs your OK".
- **AC-W4-2** Given an approved suggestion, when sent, then exactly one email per recipient is sent even if the job retries.
- **AC-W4-3** Given a staff member with open time on several days of the coming week (the feed-noise case: one gap suggestion per day), then Home shows one card for them ("Randy has 14 h open on 5 of the next 7 days"), its days listed under it, and "Dismiss all" on the card snoozes every one of them as one dismissal (it counts once towards "turn this off?"); regulars who are due are grouped into one card the same way ("4 regulars are due back"), each with their own link (S52, ADR-0043).
- **AC-W4-4** Given suggestions worth different amounts, then Home lists them by the dollars at stake, the most first, each with an estimate ("~$540"): open time at the business's average price per minute of its priced services, a regular who is due at their service's price, a client likely to miss at the booking's price; a suggestion without a price (a busy week, a business with no prices) comes after them, newest first (S52, ADR-0043).
- **AC-W4-5** Given a business with no booking history yet (no visit that started before now; imported visits count), then Home shows no suggestions, only a quiet line saying what will come once clients book and how to start; the first visit lifts it. The briefing, what waits for an OK and the to-dos show as before (S52, ADR-0043).
- **AC-W4-6** Given clients with at least 2 visits, nothing booked ahead and a last visit older than their usual gap (the last service's rebooking interval, else the median gap between their visits), then "Haven't been back" lists them, the most recently due first, each with the last visit, the last service and its price ("~$45": the service's, else what that visit cost, as imported visits carry it); a client unsubscribed from emails or without an email shows why they can't be emailed (S54.2, ADR-0057).
- **AC-W4-7** Given a client on "Haven't been back" who can be emailed, when an owner or manager taps "Draft email", then one drafted email offering their next open time (their usual staff member first, within 7 days) waits for an OK on the list and on Home; a second tap the same day returns the same draft; the business gets at most 20 such drafts a day; no draft is made for a client who unsubscribed, has no email, or had a nudge or offer in the quiet window, and the send checks the opt-out again (S54.2, ADR-0057).
- **AC-W4-8** Given a visits import that finishes, then the result says how many regulars are overdue and about how much they are worth ("12 regulars are overdue, about $540"), the same count and sum as "Haven't been back", with a link to it; no dollars when none are priced, no sentence when none are overdue (S54.2, ADR-0057).
- **AC-W4-9** Given no client is overdue, then "Haven't been back" says what will fill it ("After 2 visits, a client who stops coming shows here") with the action to import past visits (S54.2 top-up, D5).

## W5 Smart waitlist
- **AC-W5-1** Given a waitlist entry matching a cancelled slot, when the cancellation occurs, then an offer is proposed (or sent, per autonomy) to the best match within 2 minutes; the first confirmation books it and others are notified the slot is taken.
- **AC-W5-2** Given a waitlist request whose window still has a free time for that service (and staff member, if named), when anyone adds it (the app or the AI), then nothing is added and the free times are returned, so the person can book one instead.
- **AC-W5-3** Given a cancelled slot and fewer people on the waitlist who fit it than the business offers it to (3 by default), then overdue regulars of that staff member whose usual service fits the slot exactly are offered the rest, after everyone on the waitlist: only regulars with an email who haven't turned off Pepper's suggestions, have nothing booked ahead and had no such offer in the last 7 days; their email says their usual time opened up and holds the same single-use claim link (S54, ADR-0056).
- **AC-W5-4** Given cancellations on the real worker, then each slot is offered (the proposal stored or the emails queued) within 2 minutes at the 95th percentile, and no offer email ever goes to a client other than the one offered: none to a client who opted out, has no address, is booked ahead, is deleted, belongs to another staff member, or whose cancellation was undone; N concurrent claims including a regular's still make exactly one booking (S54).
- **AC-W5-5** Given an offer Pepper made, then Activity names who got it and the slot's value ("Offered Tue 2 PM to Maria and Ana, ~$60"), and once a client claims it, the line says so with the booked price ("Filled Tue 2 PM with Maria, +$60"); an offer nobody claimed says the time passed unclaimed (S54, the S53 retro's top-up).

## W6 Reminders
- **AC-W6-1** Given a booking 24 h ahead, then a reminder email is sent within 5 minutes of schedule; cancelled bookings never receive reminders.

## W7 Trust
- **AC-W7-1** Every AI action shows a "Why?" explanation listing the inputs used.
- **AC-W7-2** Deleting a memory fact removes it from all future AI context (verified by eval).
- **AC-W7-3** Actions in the `external_or_irreversible` tier can never be set to "just do it" for bulk operations (> 10 recipients).
- **AC-W7-4** Given a member on Activity (More → Activity, `/activity`), then every action Pepper took that they may see is listed, newest first, in plain words: what was done, when, who approved it, which emails it sent or held back, and whether it was undone, with Undo while its window is open; "Show older" pages back through all of them. Staff see the ones done for them (S52, ADR-0043).
- **AC-W7-5** Given an owner or manager on Autonomy, then three presets (Careful, Balanced, Hands-off) set every action in one tap, each saying in a line what it means; the page names the preset the levels match, or "your own mix"; "Customise each action" opens the 13 actions (open by default for a mix). Careful asks for everything; Balanced is the business's defaults; Hands-off never lets a message to more than 10 clients or a new booking rule run without an OK. Staff see it read-only (S53, ADR-0055).
- **AC-W7-6** Given a member on Autonomy, then "Your standing rules" reads the dial and the business-wide booking rules back as sentences (what Pepper asks before, does and tells, or just does; the two that always ask; notice, how far ahead, the daily cap and upcoming closed days), with a link to change the booking rules for those who may read them (S53).
- **AC-W7-7** Given a new business, then a move Pepper proposes waits for an OK by default, and its card says who gets the email, by name and address ("Emails Maria (maria@…) the new time"); a client the viewer may not see, or without an address, is named without one (S53).
- **AC-W7-8** Given a member on Autonomy with each action open, then every action says how many times Pepper did it in the last 30 days ("Done 12 times in the last 30 days"), from the same records as Activity; staff count what was done for them (S53, the S52 retro's top-up).
- **AC-W7-9** Given 20 actions of one kind in a row without a mistake (an Undo marked "wrong" or a card turned down restarts the run; the last 90 days), then Pepper offers an owner or manager one step more room for that kind on its row ("Ask me" → "Do it and tell me" → "Just do it"), only where the action allows it and not while "Ask me first for everything" is on; one tap saves it (S53, ADR-0055).

## W8 Public booking
- **AC-W8-1** Given two customers booking the same last slot concurrently, then exactly one succeeds and the other is offered alternatives.
- **AC-W8-2** A booking is created only after email verification; the confirmation email contains working reschedule/cancel links scoped to that booking.

- **AC-W8-3** Given the public booking menu, then a provider without working hours is excluded, and a service appears only if an active provider with hours offers it. Otherwise eligible providers are not excluded merely because the viewed range is fully booked or covered by time off. Internal staff visibility, staff permissions and domain booking checks remain unchanged.
- **AC-W8-4** Given a selected service/provider and the displayed booking date range, when the time picker first loads or those selections change, then the first day in that range with a valid free slot is selected using the business's zone and existing booking rules. No times in that range is described as a range limitation, not as missing setup or no availability ever; the waitlist path remains available. Failed or incomplete/truncated data cannot establish a full day. Late results never override a newer selection or the customer's deliberately chosen date; the search and request count are bounded.
- **AC-W8-5** Given an availability request fails, when the customer chooses "Try again", then availability is retried for the same service/provider/date with accessible loading and error feedback, retained customer inputs, no duplicate submission, and no stale response overriding newer selections.
- **AC-W8-6** Given a public booking or setup time-zone label, then it uses an accurate plain name (e.g. "Central Time") or meaningful fallback while the stored zone and rendered business-local dates/times are unchanged; zones with different DST behavior are not incorrectly equated.
- **AC-W8-7** Given a business with a phone, a public address or a cancellation policy, then the booking page shows each one that is set (the phone as a call link), and the policy on the details step and the confirmation; none set shows nothing empty. Owners and managers edit them with the booking page's color; staff can't; invalid values are refused with what to fix; the mailing address is never shown.
- **AC-W8-8** Given a client who confirmed a booking code with "Remember me on this phone" ticked, when they book the same business again from that browser with the same email within 90 days of its last use, then the booking is made without a code, and the page says so. Another email, another business, an expired, forgotten or altered token, or the box unticked still asks for a code; the token is kept only as a hash, and "Forget this device" on manage-booking ends it.
- **AC-W8-9** Given a visit to the booking page, when the client enters a step (service, staff, time, details, verify, done, waitlisted), then that step is recorded once per visit with a random visit id, the step and the time only (no name, email, phone or address); the counts per step are readable by the business only; a failed record never blocks booking.
- **AC-W8-10** Given an owner or manager, when they add a logo or a cover photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, at most 10 MB; a logo at least 128 px on its short side, a cover at least 600 px), then it is checked by its content (not its name or label), turned upright, stripped of its metadata (location included), resized on the server and shown on the booking page; a new one replaces the old; wrong type, too large, too small or too many pixels is refused with what to fix; staff can't change photos.
- **AC-W8-11** Given the gallery, then it holds up to 12 photos of the business's work (same checks, at least 600 px), each with an optional caption (at most 150 characters); the 13th is refused with "The gallery holds 12 photos"; removing one deletes its stored images.
- **AC-W8-12** Given a business with photos, when a client opens the booking page, then the cover is on top, the logo beside the name (the initials otherwise) and the gallery on the services step, each with alt text (its caption, or "Logo of <business>", "<business>", "Work at <business>"); images come from the app's own origin, cached as unchanging, sized for the screen; a business without photos looks as before; one business's photo can't be read through another's page.
- **AC-W8-13** Given a bookable business with photos, when a new client on a 375 px phone picks a service, "Anyone available" and the first free time, enters their name and email and confirms with the emailed code, then their time by the owner time model is under 2 minutes (the email's wait and the trips to the mail app counted); booking again from the same remembered phone (no code) is under 60 seconds.

## W9 Team
- **AC-W9-1** A staff user can see and edit only their own bookings and notes (enforced at API and RLS level).

## Cross-cutting
- **AC-X-1** No user can read or write another tenant's data through UI, API, or AI (cross-tenant suite).
- **AC-X-2** If the LLM provider is unavailable, all non-AI screens remain fully functional.
